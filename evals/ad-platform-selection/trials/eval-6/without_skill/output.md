## Podcast Sponsorship Plan — Vantabyte ($9K/month, year-long, no deadline)

**Core call: concentrate, don't spread.** Podcast response is frequency-driven — a listener needs 3–5 exposures before an ad registers, let alone converts. $9K split across five shows buys forgettable cameos. $9K on two shows, run weekly, for a full quarter, buys recall. With no deadline pressure, use that patience to run this as a proper test-and-scale program instead of a spray.

Staggering the second show's start matters — if both launch simultaneously, you can't tell which one is driving a signup bump.

### Format rules

- **Host-read only.** Never dynamic/programmatic insertion. A technical audience tunes out generic ad reads in the first two seconds; a host who actually describes their own on-call pain converts multiples better.
- **Mid-roll placement.** Highest-attention slot. Pre-roll is cheaper but gets skipped/muted far more.
- **60–90 seconds, lead with the pain, not the brand.** You invented this category — nobody's searching for "on-call incident tool." Open with the 3am page / the MTTR spreadsheet / the on-call rotation nobody wants, *then* name Vantabyte. Educate the category before you sell the product.
- **Rotate 2–3 scripts every 4–6 weeks.** Same small pool of repeat listeners will fatigue fast on one script.
- **Unique vanity URL + promo code per show** (e.g. `vantabyte.com/screaming`, `vantabyte.com/sed`) for attribution — but see measurement note below, because code-based tracking alone will undercount.

### Measurement

Podcast attribution is famously leaky — people listen on a run or a commute and convert later, on a different device, without the code. Track three signals together, not just the code:

1. **Promo-code signups** (hard floor, definitely undercounts)
2. **"How did you hear about us?"** field at signup — catches the delayed-conversion, code-forgotten crowd
3. **Branded search volume** (Search Console) as a lift indicator, even for people who never click through

**Breakeven framing:** at $120/mo and 85% margin (~$102/customer/month), $9K/month needs roughly **88 new paying customers/month** to be self-funding purely from this channel. Track weekly signups-per-show against that number, not downloads or impressions — downloads are vanity for a self-serve B2B tool with this price point.

### Deal structure — use your lack of urgency as leverage

- Don't buy annual upfront on an unproven show. Run the 13-week test at standard rates first.
- Once a show proves out, **negotiate an annual rate** (typically 15–20% off month-to-month) — you're a low-risk, no-deadline buyer, which is exactly the kind of advertiser networks like to lock in.
- Ask for **category exclusivity** (no other on-call/incident tool sponsoring concurrently) written into the renewal.
- Ask what's bundled — most of these networks (especially Changelog Media) package a newsletter mention or social post with the audio buy at little/no extra cost. Always ask, rarely offered by default.
