- Last-click on 9/11 deals tells you which channel closed the deal, not which channel created the demand. n=11 is also too small to lean on hard.
- Check branded vs. non-branded search terms on those 9. If most are branded ("Ferrostat", "[you] vs [competitor]"), that's someone who already decided to buy — search just captured the last touch. That's not evidence search *generates* pipeline.

- Industrial IoT monitoring is a narrow keyword universe. Going straight to 100%/$20K in search likely won't 4x your search volume — it'll bid up your own CPCs against a fixed pool of searchers, with diminishing returns.
