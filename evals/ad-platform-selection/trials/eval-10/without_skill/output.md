# Tidewatch Alliance — DE/FR/NL Awareness Campaign, €25,000 / 3 Months

## Before anything else: the 2023 playbook doesn't transfer cleanly

Running "the same plan as 2023" carries a real compliance risk in 2026 that didn't exist then. The **EU Regulation on the Transparency and Targeting of Political Advertising (TTPA, Reg. 2024/900)** became fully applicable on 10 October 2025. It defines "political advertising" broadly — any message "liable to influence" the outcome of a legislative or regulatory process, not just elections. A campaign explicitly timed to sway an EU Parliament vote is squarely inside that definition.

Consequences to verify before you commit spend:

- **Meta** announced it would stop accepting social-issue, electoral, and political ads across the EU in response to TTPA, effective around the October 2025 application date. As of my last knowledge (Jan 2026), Facebook/Instagram may simply reject vote-linked creative in DE/FR/NL — confirm current status in Meta's Business Help Center before allocating budget there.
- **Google** signaled similar restrictions on political advertising in the EU around the same timeline. Confirm current Google Ads EU political-content policy directly.
- Any ad that _is_ accepted as political advertising under TTPA requires: a sponsor-transparency notice, published spend/targeting data in the EU ad repository, and a ban on targeting via special-category data (political opinions, etc.).

**Action item #1, this week:** have Tidewatch's legal/compliance contact confirm current Meta and Google policy pages for DE, FR, NL, and decide whether campaign creative references the vote explicitly or stays framed as general ocean-awareness content (lower regulatory risk, weaker direct pressure). I could not verify live platform policy for this response — treat the numbers below as the media plan, not as confirmation that Meta/Google will serve it as written.

The plan below is built to survive either outcome: a resilient core that doesn't depend on Meta/Google accepting vote-linked ads, plus a small gated test budget for paid social if compliance clears it.

## Timeline — 12 weeks, phased against the vote date (V)

**Phase 1, Weeks 1–4 (V-12 to V-9): Build**

- Launch YouTube + display, broad reach, build retargeting pools.
- Publish action page (petition / "contact your MEP" tool).
- Lock creator partnerships, seed PR/earned media.

**Phase 2, Weeks 5–8 (V-8 to V-5): Pressure**

- Intensify search + native, retarget Phase 1 viewers.
- Push MEP-contact tool hard; creators publish UGC.
- First DOOH burst in Brussels.

**Phase 3, Weeks 9–12 (V-4 to vote week): Mobilize**

- Heaviest spend in vote week itself.
- DOOH in Strasbourg/Brussels during plenary.
- Rapid-response creative reacting to news/lobbying counter-moves.
- Post-vote: results/thank-you content to the list you built (owned channel, zero incremental ad spend, high retention value for the next fight).

## Targeting notes per market

- **Germany**: broad urban + coastal (Kiel, Hamburg, Rostock), plus policy/media audiences in Berlin. German audiences respond well to data-led, factual creative.
- **France**: Brittany and coastal départements carry the fishing-industry counter-narrative — expect more contested comment sections; pair paid with active community moderation. Paris for policy/media reach.
- **Netherlands**: nationwide reach is cheap; layer in a dedicated push toward Dutch fishing communities (IJmuiden, Urk) with more nuanced messaging — this is the market most likely to have organized opposition.

## KPIs

- Cost per petition signature / MEP contact generated (primary metric — not just reach).
- Video completion rate (YouTube, target >50% for 15s assets).
- Media mentions / share of voice vs. fishing-industry lobby messaging.
- Post-vote: correlate campaign-heavy constituencies against how their MEPs voted, for the after-action report.
