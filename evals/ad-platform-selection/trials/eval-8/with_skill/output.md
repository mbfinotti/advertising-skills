## Why "start cheap on CTV" doesn't work

**1. Your contribution margin can't carry connected TV, at any price.**
Contribution per sale = $38 × 34% = **$12.92**. Connected TV's own disqualifier rule: *"CPM plus production math rarely clears a sub-$50 contribution."* You're at $13. This isn't a budget problem — it fails outright regardless of how cheap the entry tier is.

**2. The $500/month "minimum" is a billing floor, not a signal threshold.**
The vendor's own guidance for a readable test is ~$165/day (~$5,000/month), run for a month. $500/month at $20–40 CPMs buys 12,500–25,000 impressions — not a test, a rounding error. And CTV is "structurally weak at the click level" by design, so you'd also need an incrementality/geo-lift build to read it at all — a standing measurement job, not a "start this week" move.

**3. Podcast fails on its own arithmetic before you even get there.**
Measurable podcast spend needs ~$15,000+ spread across 3+ shows in a multi-episode flight. Your *entire* monthly budget is $14K. Adding it "later" doesn't change that it needs more than your whole budget just to be readable.

**4. Verdora's mix tells you nothing about your mix.**
Copying a competitor's channels without matching their economics is the single most common way these plans fail. Verdora being "all over" CTV/podcast says nothing about their margin, AOV, or budget relative to yours — those channels have the highest floors and slowest signal of any family. Whatever lets Verdora afford them, you don't have it yet at $14K/month and 34% margin.
