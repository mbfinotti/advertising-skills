## 4. Primary recommendation

**Paid search.** Demand already exists for "webinar software" as a search category, the format (text + landing page) matches your actual production capacity, and the affordability math clears with large headroom ($67 avg CPL vs. $889 allowable CAC). It's also the only surviving family with a fast, honest time-to-effect.

## 5. Funding plan

- Budget: $7,000/month.
- Paid search's own floor is tiny (10 clicks/day, 3-5x target CPA) — this budget massively overfunds a search-only spend.
- **Split: ~$5,600/month (80%) to paid search**, **~$1,400/month to a price-discovery test on paid review listings** (comfortably clears the $500-$3,000/month entry floors for G2/Capterra). This isn't the "split five ways and starve everything" anti-pattern — both lines individually clear their own signal thresholds.

## 6. Test design

**Paid search:**
1. Price discovery: ~$100 on exact-match category terms.
2. Real test: 3-5x target CPA per concept = $2,667-$4,445 per concept (at the $889 target), run 2-4 weeks, same weekday start/end.
3. Pass: CPL ≤ $889 at 90-95% confidence.
4. Kill: 2-3x target CPA spent ($1,778-$2,667), zero conversions, after a readable sample.

**Timeline — the number you actually asked for:**
- Week 1: account + landing page setup (confirm tracking is live first — unverified tracking voids the whole test).
- Days after launch: price discovery numbers in.
- Weeks 2-5: real test window.
- **First readable pass/fail signal: ~3-5 weeks from launch** — call it **mid-to-late October 2026** if you start this week.

**If you still want to test TikTok/Reels despite the gate:** the honest timeline is "not yet." You'd need to stand up video production capacity first (in-house hire or contractor producing 10-20 variants/month) — that's a separate initiative measured in weeks-to-months before a single ad flight starts, and only then does the platform's own learning window (comparable order of magnitude to paid social's ~50 events/ad set/7 days) begin. Running it now with 2 statics/month doesn't produce a slow result — it produces no readable result at all, because the ad sets never accumulate enough fresh creative to exit learning.

## 7. Second-channel triggers

Add paid review listings' full spend (beyond the $1,400 test) once its own price-discovery test returns a real CPL under $889. Add anything beyond that only when paid search shows saturation (rising CPA, impression-share ceiling) — not on a calendar, and never by re-splitting the same $7K thinner.

## 8. Measurement approach

- Default rung only: UTM discipline + last-non-direct. No creation channel is in this mix yet, so self-reported attribution isn't needed until one is (e.g., if you later stand up video and revisit short-form video).
- **Verify conversion tracking end-to-end before any spend** — this wasn't confirmed in your brief and it's the one thing that silently voids the whole test.

**Bottom line:** the $7K/month you were about to put into TikTok/Reels can't produce a result on the current creative capacity — that's a hard capacity gate, not a patience problem. Redirect it to paid search (+ a small review-listings test) and expect a first statistically-readable signal in **3-5 weeks**.
