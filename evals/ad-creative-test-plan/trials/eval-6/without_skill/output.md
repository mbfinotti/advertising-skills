# Trailhead Test Plan — Nordvik Outdoor Paid Social

Two new video concepts vs. the control. This is your plan with two corrections — without them, the "winner" you pick has a real chance of being noise, not signal.

**Setup:** 3 cells (Concept A, Concept B, Control) · $250/day/cell · $45 target CPA · ~5.6 est. conversions/cell/day.

## Two fixes before this goes live

**Fix 1 — Stop checking every morning and stopping at the first 95%.**
Each daily peek is another chance to hit 95% by luck. Check for four straight mornings and your real false-positive rate is well above the 5% that "95%" promises — often 20–30%+. This is the single most common way creative tests produce a wrong winner.
→ Keep watching spend pacing and delivery daily, that's just ops hygiene. But make the significance call **once**, on a pre-set date, not on whichever morning it happens to clear 95%.

**Fix 2 — Control launches 4 days after the variants.**
A/B run Thu–Sun, Control starts Monday. Different days of week, different auction pressure, different point in each ad's learning phase. Any CPA gap measured before all three have run concurrently is comparing conditions, not creative.
→ Treat Thu–Sun as pre-flight burn-in only for A/B — free learning-phase progress, no comparison. The test clock, and the 14-day window below, starts Monday once Control joins.

## Official protocol

1. **Launch — Thu, Sep 17.** Concept A and Concept B go live at $250/day each. No comparison, no CPA calls, no pausing this week.
2. **Control joins — Mon, Sep 21.** Control goes live at $250/day under its new post IDs. This is Day 1 of the test clock for all three cells.
3. **Daily ops check (not a decision point).** Confirm each cell is spending, delivering, not flagged for policy or fatigue. Log raw CPA if useful — don't act on it.
4. **First valid read — Mon, Oct 5 (Day 14).** ~78 conversions/cell by then — clears the ~50-conversion learning-phase threshold and spans two full weekends. Run the significance test once, here.
5. **Decide.** Reallocate budget only if a cell leads at 95% significance **and** has ≥50 conversions **and** has exited learning phase — all three, not just the p-value.
6. **If it's still close — extend to Mon, Oct 12 (Day 21).** One extension, maximum. Re-checking weekly past that reopens the peeking problem Fix 1 closed.

## Timeline

`Thu 9/17` A+B live (burn-in) → `Mon 9/21` Control live, Day 1 → `Mon 10/5` Day 14, first valid check → `Mon 10/12` Day 21, extension deadline.

## Stop-loss (separate from the significance call)

If a cell's trailing 3-day CPA tops **$90** (2× target) after logging at least 20 conversions, pause it for cost control at any time. Log it as a budget decision, not a "loser" verdict — it hasn't run long enough to earn one.
