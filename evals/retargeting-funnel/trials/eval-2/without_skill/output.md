# Remarketing Window Recommendations — Corvid Systems

No time-to-conversion report needed for this — these are the standard defaults used across B2B SaaS/compliance vendors with multi-month cycles, calibrated to your 4–6 month deal length.

## The three audiences

**1. Site visitors (all traffic)**

- Window: **30 days**
- Rationale: broad top-of-funnel pool, refreshed monthly. This is the universal default regardless of vertical — casts wide, decays fast, cheap to run.

**2. Feature page viewers**

- Window: **60 days**
- Rationale: mid-funnel intent signal (they looked at capabilities, not just the homepage). Standard practice extends this beyond the 30-day default because feature-page research in banking compliance tends to span multiple stakeholders and sessions before anyone requests a demo.

**3. Demo requesters**

- Window: **180 days** (or your platform's max, whichever is longer)
- Rationale: this is your only true bottom-funnel signal, and it should span the whole deal cycle. With 4–6 months average and a security review mid-cycle, a 90-day window would drop these people from remarketing before the deal even reaches the security-review stage.

## Platform caps to know

| Platform                             | Max membership duration |
| ------------------------------------ | ----------------------- |
| Google Ads (remarketing lists)       | 540 days                |
| Meta (Custom Audiences from website) | 180 days                |
| LinkedIn (Matched Audiences)         | 365 days                |
| X/Twitter                            | 365 days                |

Meta's 180-day cap is your binding constraint for the demo-requester audience — set it to the max there, and mirror 180 days on Google/LinkedIn for consistency even though they allow longer.

## One structural flag, not a window issue

Don't run generic prospecting creative against demo requesters once they're an active opportunity — suppress them from broad remarketing and route them into your ABM/nurture flow instead (security-review case studies, compliance-audit content). Mixing "come back and see our features" ads with a prospect who's mid-security-review reads as tone-deaf and wastes spend on someone sales already owns.

## Caveat

These are industry-standard defaults, not your funnel's actual decay curve. Once you have 60–90 days of CRM data, pull actual visitor-to-demo and demo-to-close timing and tighten the feature-page window (it's the one most likely to be over- or under-set) — but these numbers are safe to launch on Friday.
