# Mossvale Robotics — LinkedIn Promotion Campaign: Post Selection

**Criteria used:** relevance to the ICP (warehouse ops/logistics buyers, not investors/talent), engagement *quality* (comments > reactions as a resonance signal), performance vs. the 120-reaction median, and fit as a *paid* unit (native-feeling formats beat company-news formats as ads).

## Verdict by post

**✅ (c) Customer picking-error story — PROMOTE (top pick).**
210 reactions / 40 comments = ~19% comment ratio, the strongest resonance signal in the set, on a topic the ICP directly recognizes as their own pain. Plain-text story format reads as native content in-feed, not an ad — the best-performing shape for paid LinkedIn.

**✅ (e) PDF cost teardown — PROMOTE (second pick).**
1,500 reactions, well above median; document posts get strong dwell-time/algorithm treatment and this one is the actual ROI argument (cost of warehouse ops) that maps to the sales conversation. Evergreen, reusable asset.

**❌ (a) Series B carousel — EXCLUDE.**
3,100 reactions is inflated by investors/press/talent congratulating a funding round, not buyers engaging with a problem. Self-congratulatory framing reads as brand noise to a cold ICP audience. Repurpose for recruiting or investor-relations retargeting instead — not this campaign.

**⏳ (b) Poll on automation pain points — HOLD, don't boost as-is.**
Votes are a low-effort tap, not proof a cold audience will act on it as an ad. More valuable as research input — feed its findings into the two ghostwritten posts — than as a promoted unit itself.

**❌ (d) Robotic arm launch pitch — EXCLUDE.**
95 reactions is below median: the organic audience already told you the hook/creative isn't landing. Paying to amplify an underperforming pitch just proves that louder. Send it back to the content lead as a rewrite candidate, not a promotion candidate.

**⏳ (f) Hiring philosophy video — HOLD, revisit in a few days.**
Strong early velocity (5x median in one day) but it serves employer brand/recruiting, not buyer demand gen — wrong audience/KPI for this campaign. Also still climbing organically; boosting mid-climb would cut its own organic reach short. Don't decide until it plateaus, and only reconsider it if a recruiting-focused budget line exists separately.

## Campaign roster

| Asset | Role |
|---|---|
| (c) Picking-error story | Paid social proof — pain-point resonance |
| (e) Cost teardown PDF | Paid social proof — ROI/authority |
| Ghostwritten post #1 | Primary conversion asset (purpose-built CTA) |

Treat the two ghostwritten posts as the campaign's primary assets, not supplements — they're built for a paid CTA from the start, which organic posts weren't. Brief the content lead to draw on (b)'s poll findings and (c)'s pain-point framing so both new posts target the same ICP problem, not a fourth unrelated topic. (a), (d), and (f) stay out of this campaign.
