# Ferrowdyne — Thought Leadership Ads: Diagnosis & Revised Plan

**Verdict: don't double the budget. The problem isn't spend — it's a ~24x audience-to-budget mismatch, plus at least one missing prerequisite that needs answering before another dollar goes in.**

## Rationale block (why, in one place)

- Sequence: trust-first (default for a person-fronted B2B format) — confirm the COO's LinkedIn following doesn't already outrank Ferrowdyne's brand account; if it does, keep trust-first anyway.
- Root cause: frequency near zero, not creative or offer.
- Gate failed today: **budget-cannot-sustain-frequency** (Eligibility gate). Possibly also **no downstream capture layer** — unverified, see below.
- Fix direction: shrink audience to fit budget, not raise budget to fit audience.

## Two things I can't verify from what you've told me — answer these before locking the plan

1. **ACV and sales cycle.** This format's practitioner-cited justification bar is ~$25k+ ACV with a narrow ICP, because payback is slow (90+ days, often a year+). Below that, this budget belongs in direct-response/retargeting instead, not here.
2. **Does a retargeting/conversion-tracking layer already exist for this campaign?** Engagement not captured is unrecoverable — it doesn't backfill later. If nothing is capturing post-engagers or landing-page visitors today, the 8 weeks of spend has built nothing durable regardless of what we fix below. This has to exist before any more spend, not after.

Everything below assumes both check out. If either doesn't, stop here and fix that first.

## Root cause: frequency math, not budget size

| | Current | CEO's fix ($5k/mo) | Recommended |
|---|---|---|---|
| Audience | 310,000 (full ICP) | 310,000 | ~20,000–40,000 (layered) |
| Monthly budget | $2,400 | $5,000 | $2,400 (unchanged) |
| Est. monthly impressions* | ~40,000 | ~83,000 | ~40,000 |
| Est. monthly frequency | **~0.13x** | **~0.27x** | **~1.3–2x** |

*Illustrative only, built from published CPC/CTR figures (~$60 CPM implied) — replace with your account's actual delivered-impressions and frequency numbers from Campaign Manager, which outrank any published estimate.

At 0.13x monthly frequency, roughly 1 in 8 of your 310,000-person audience sees the ad *once* in a month — most never see it at all. This is the textbook failure mode for this format: "spending $2k/month against 300k people, no one's seeing your stuff." Doubling to $5k still leaves frequency under 1x — you'd still be buying near-zero reach against the same oversized list. **Widen-the-list-before-adding-budget only applies once a list is saturated; you're nowhere near that. Shrink first.**

Published guidance for sub-$10k/month budgets targets a 20,000–50,000-person audience. Get there by layering, not by picking a random slice:

- Start from a matched target-account list (your actual ICP companies), not "all logistics professionals."
- Layer job function (ops/supply chain/procurement) + seniority (your actual decision-maker level) on top.
- If you have a named target-account list, consider going narrower still — 500–2,000 contacts is a workable named-account band and would let $2,400/month buy real frequency.

Run at the current $2,400/month against the resized list for the 4–6 week floor before revisiting budget at all.

## Audience exclusions — yes, and it's overdue, not optional

Right now nothing in what you've described suggests any exclusions exist. That means your 1,900 customers and 40 employees have likely been eating impressions inside that 310k for 8 weeks — wasted twice: spend, and credibility (showing a customer/employee a "look at our impact" ad they already know is paid).

Build in this order — first two tie, need no platform involvement, ship today:

1. **Existing customers** (1,900) — CRM export, upload as excluded list.
2. **Open pipeline** — same CRM export motion, same priority.
3. **Employees** (40) — company domain/HRIS list, or LinkedIn's employee-exclusion setting.
4. **Competitors** — build a short named list if you don't have one; not mentioned as existing.
5. **Students** — lowest priority for this ICP, skip unless you're seeing noise.

**Delete, don't wait on: site-visitor URL exclusion.** Your rep says it's not enabled at your tier — that's a real capability ceiling, not a queue position. Take it off the plan now and say why, rather than letting it sit as phantom "coming soon" work that never lands. Separately, ask what tier/cost unlocks it and treat that as its own decision — not a blocker to shipping exclusions 1–4 this week.

## Other things worth checking while you're in there

- **Link/CTA plan.** LinkedIn's format carries no advertiser-added link — a click destination only exists if the COO put one in the post (pinned comment, or an edited-in tracked link). If nothing's there, "basically nothing" includes zero attributable clicks by design, independent of the frequency problem. Confirm which of the two exists; if neither, that's the "no link anywhere" failure mode and needs fixing regardless of audience/budget changes.
- **Single-person program.** Only the COO is featured — flag, not a blocker: one revocation, one busy quarter, or a departure ends the whole program. Consider adding a second voice (another exec, or a customer champion) so it isn't hostage to one person's calendar.
- **Written agreement.** Confirm there's something beyond platform click-to-approve covering term, spend caps, who can edit the post, and revocation handling — not just informal buy-in.
- **How "basically nothing" was measured.** If that verdict came from reported CTR or engagement rate, that's the wrong signal — the largest published dataset found reported CTR *negatively* correlated with pipeline (rho = -0.170). Re-check cost per landing-page click and retargeting-pool growth instead; those are what should actually decide this.

## Measurement going forward

You're past the point where you should have real data (learning window is 7–14 days; you're at 8 weeks). Run the gate now instead of guessing from impressions:

- **Gate 1:** cost per landing-page click ≤ your own brand-account ads' cost per click to a comparable audience. If you don't have that baseline, use $3–5 as a provisional bar and label it provisional.
- **Gate 2:** engager retargeting pool has crossed your platform's minimum serveable floor (commonly ~300 matched members — verify current number).
- If gate 1 fails, rotate the post, not the audience. If gate 2 fails, audience/budget is the problem — which, per above, is where you already are.
- Never quote the doubled-budget idea, or any future review, against CTR.

## Bottom line for the CEO conversation

- Doubling budget without shrinking the audience buys more of the same near-zero frequency — it won't move the needle.
- Fix audience sizing first (310k → ~20-40k, layered), ship the four exclusions you can build today, confirm the link/CTA and tracking prerequisites, then re-run the current $2,400/month for another 4–6 weeks and judge it on cost-per-landing-page-click and pool growth — not on gut feel or CTR.
- Only talk about more budget once that resized list is actually seeing meaningful frequency and gate 1/2 data says it's worth scaling.
