**Verdict: No — the sequencing solves the wrong problem.**

**Why the playbook doesn't transfer**

The "cold thought-leader posts → retarget engagers with brand ads" pattern exists to solve *trust scarcity*: nobody knows the company, so a human voice earns the first handshake, and brand ads close the loop once familiarity exists. Novantis Grid doesn't have that problem. 40k company followers in a "well-known name in the space" means ICP trust in the brand already exists — it's what your always-on cold campaigns are built on and what's already filling retargeting pools.

What you actually have is a *different* asset: a new academic CTO whose posts are sharp but unproven at scale. Running her cold to ICP treats her like the trust-builder for an unknown company. She isn't that. She's a credibility/differentiation signal layered *on top of* an already-trusted brand — closer to an analyst-relations or ABM asset than a top-of-funnel one.

**Three concrete failure modes in the agency's plan**

1. **Duplicate machinery, split budget.** You already have a cold-to-warm engine running (always-on brand campaigns → retargeting). Adding a second cold-to-warm engine off her personal content doesn't sequence with the first — it competes with it for the same ICP attention and budget, with no coordination between which cold touch a prospect sees first.
2. **Cold + unknown = weak signal.** A 900-follower academic voice, pushed cold with no brand context, gives the audience no reason to trust the opinion yet. Cold engagement/CPMs on an unfamiliar personal account will underperform your proven brand creative — you'd be paying acquisition prices for content that hasn't earned organic pull yet.
3. **Wrong stage for the format.** Personal thought-leadership content converts hardest at consideration/decision (dispels "is this vendor actually cutting-edge," reassures technical evaluators, works ABM committees) — not at cold awareness, where brand recognition already does the job better.

**What actually sequences correctly**

- Keep the always-on cold brand campaigns exactly as they are — that's your proven top-of-funnel, don't touch it.
- Feed her posts to your *existing* warm pools first: retargeting audiences, existing customers, and named ABM accounts. That's where "we just hired a brilliant academic CTO" is a differentiation story that moves a buying committee, not a stranger's opinion.
- Let her grow organically off that warm base for ~60–90 days. Watch follower growth, engagement rate, and whether her content shifts consideration-stage metrics (demo requests, content downloads from warm segments) versus a holdout.
- Only then test narrow paid amplification (e.g., Thought Leader Ads) — targeted at the same ICP segments already in your retargeting pool or named accounts, not a fresh cold blast. Earn the right to go broad with her voice before spending media dollars on it.

**One-line reframe for the agency:** her content isn't the top of your funnel — it's a credibility layer for the warm middle. Sequence her downstream of brand, not upstream of it.
