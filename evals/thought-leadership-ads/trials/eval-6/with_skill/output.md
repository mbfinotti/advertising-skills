No — the sequencing is backwards for this specific setup, and it's backwards in a way that would waste real budget. Two things the proposal gets wrong:

**1. The default flips on recognition, and yours has flipped.**
The skill's rule: trust-first (cold-to-ICP on the person, then brand ads to engagers) is right *when the featured person outranks the brand in recognition* — the classic early-stage founder case. Awareness-first (cold brand ads first, person-fronted ads mid-funnel, conversion ask last) is right *when the brand outranks the person*. Novantis Grid is an established name with 40k company followers; your CTO is a week-one academic hire with 900. That's not a close call — the brand carries far more trust equity than she does right now. Running her posts cold to your ICP asks total strangers to extend trust to an unfamiliar face, when the thing they'd actually recognize (your brand) is sitting unused in the wings. That's the opposite of what a cold impression should be doing.

**2. The proposal builds a redundant, weaker cold layer instead of using the one you already have.**
The only case where you'd add a cold layer on top of an already-outranking brand is "we need a cold prospecting layer anyway to seed retargeting pools, so the extra layer earns its keep twice." You don't have that gap — your always-on cold brand campaigns are already doing that job and, by your own account, feeding healthy retargeting pools. Sending her posts cold duplicates infrastructure that already works, and does it with a weaker asset (900 followers, no engagement history to score against yet).

**Corrected sequence (awareness-first):**
1. Keep the always-on cold brand campaigns running as-is — they're already the awareness layer.
2. Serve the CTO's person-fronted posts to the *engagers of those existing cold campaigns* — people who already have brand context — not to a fresh cold ICP pull. This is the mid-funnel trust layer, and it's cheaper and more credible against a warm audience than a cold one.
3. Retarget engagers of her posts with conversion-capable brand ads for the direct ask.

**One real tension worth surfacing before you lock this in:** if the underlying goal isn't just this flight's pipeline but building her into a compounding personal-brand asset — her own following, independent of the company — that objective pulls toward trust-first regardless of the recognition gap. Recognition and compounding-mandate pull in opposite directions here; the skill doesn't resolve that automatically, you have to state which one you're optimizing for. If it's pipeline efficiency, awareness-first wins outright. If it's "make her a name," accept that you're paying a recognition tax to do it and size the cold spend accordingly — don't let the agency present that tradeoff as if it were free.

**Two things that hold regardless of which sequence you run:**
- This format carries no conversion objective on professional networks. Have her reference the company in some posts and keep themes aligned with brand messaging — the bridge has to be deliberate or the pipeline math never closes.
- Build every retargeting audience (her post engagers, video viewers, site visitors) before the first paid impression serves. Anything not captured from day one is gone for good.

**Before this is a runnable plan, not just a corrected sequence, confirm:**
- Has she actually consented to paid promotion of her personal posts, in writing, beyond platform click-to-approve? A single-person program is also a single point of failure — one resignation and the whole channel goes dark.
- ACV and sales-cycle length — the trust-layer format only pays back around $25k+ ACV with a narrow ICP; below that, this budget belongs in direct-response and retargeting instead.
- Whether the retargeting-pool infrastructure that already serves your brand campaigns can cleanly capture *her* post engagers as a separate, taggable audience, not just get folded into the general pool.

So: reject the agency's sequencing as written. The fix isn't a tweak to their plan — trust-first is the wrong default here, full stop, unless you tell me the objective is explicitly to build her personal following over pipeline efficiency.
