## Your two weeks — prioritized, not a checklist

A growth-marketer round is dominated by the **live audit**, not the portfolio piece — this skill's format ordering is live audit > metric diagnosis > mental maths > behavioural stories > take-home polish, and a hard two-week deadline pushes you toward the formats you can drill fast, away from anything slow to build. That deletes one option outright: building fresh self-funded ad spend as evidence. There's no time for it to produce a real result in two weeks — don't let it linger on your list as a "nice to have," it's off the menu for this interview.

1. **Days 1-2:** Finish the case study above (yours already exists — this is a rewrite, not new work, so it's cheap).
2. **Days 3-10, daily:** Rehearse a live audit out loud, 10-minute structure: orient on the business → where spend concentrates and the biggest waste line → structure/tracking/bidding sanity → top 3 prioritized recommendations with the metric that proves each → what data you'd want next. Use a public account or example account, never the NDA'd one, even for practice — don't get in the habit of pulling it up.
3. **Days 3-10, daily, 15 min:** Drill the funnel-math chain out loud — impressions → CTR → clicks → CPC → spend → conv. rate → conversions → CPA → AOV → revenue → margin — until you can say intermediate numbers fluently under time pressure.
4. **One evening:** Bank 3-4 behavioural stories (ownership, conflict/client pushback, a failure, a result) in three lengths each — 2 min, 60 sec, one line.
5. **Last 2-3 days:** Run one full mock live-audit + case-study walkthrough with a friend or camera, timed.

If the process includes a take-home, time-box it to 2-4 hours and say so in the deliverable — don't sink your remaining evenings into it; that format carries the least of the hiring decision here despite being the most tempting to over-invest in.
