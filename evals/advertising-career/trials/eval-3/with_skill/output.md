## Certifications: reorder and cut

- **Efficiency:** Google Skillshop > LinkedIn Marketing Labs > TikTok Academy == Amazon Ads > Meta Blueprint
- **Value:** near-identical and low across all five — none moves a hiring decision on its own
- **Effort:** Meta Blueprint (paid exam on top of the study) > the other four (tied — about an hour of open-book exam each, plus a yearly renewal)

Four of your six are free and take roughly an hour apiece: Google Skillshop (75 min, open-book, 80% pass, 12-month expiry), LinkedIn Marketing Labs, TikTok Academy, Amazon Ads. Do all four — that's a weekend, not a season.

Meta Blueprint is the one that costs you: ~$150/exam, so your $450 covers three. It earns its exam only when a specific target listing names it or an agency's Meta partner status depends on it. You have neither yet. **Delete it from the plan now, don't leave it ranked last** — it comes back as an unfunded line item otherwise. That's $450 and 3-4 weeks recovered.

None of the six — including the four free ones — should occupy the space a results portfolio fills. Certifications teach the vendor's product the way the vendor wants it used, not how to run a profitable campaign, and the whole certification menu ranks below one published teardown. "Cert-only CV" is a documented rejection pattern, not a hypothetical risk.

## What should fill months 1-5 instead

Entry-path ordering by efficiency: agency-junior seat > self-funded own spend > lateral move > in-house coordinator > apprenticeship > freelance-first. You're not in an adjacent seat (retail management isn't SEO/analytics/account management/ecommerce ops), so the lateral-move promotion doesn't apply — though your store P&L and team-management track record is good raw material for the behavioural interview round later. Freelance-first is what this ordering starves (highest ceiling, longest runway) — promote it only if you already have a client who's said yes, or you're short on autonomy rather than reps and can afford to drop income for a period; neither applies to you yet, so leave it off the menu.

Run these in parallel from week 1, not after the certs:

1. **Apply to agency-junior/PPC-executive roles starting now.** UK postings cluster £24,000-£30,000 (itjobswatch-listed postings, 2025) — illustrative only; tell me your geography for a real anchor.
