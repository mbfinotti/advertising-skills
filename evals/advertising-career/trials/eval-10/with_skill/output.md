## Where you actually sit on the ladder

Your title says mid-level, but $180K/month of spend under one manager is senior-rung scope in most orgs — titles lag scope more often than the reverse. That gap is your leverage, not a problem: it's the raw material for a promotion case (below), _if_ you can point to an owned number, not just spend size.

Two thresholds gate the next two rungs — no course substitutes for either:

- **Budget/forecast responsibility** = the senior-track signal. If your director isn't handing this over, you're not on the senior track there, whatever the courses teach you.
- **A completed incrementality test** (geo holdout or conversion lift) = the lead/head-of-growth signal — the skill's own benchmark places this "expected" above ~$10M/yr spend. You're at ~$2.16M/yr. **Your first test will likely be underpowered.** Run it anyway — as a directional/portfolio piece, not a statistically bulletproof one — and plan to run a real one at whatever employer eventually spends $10M+.
