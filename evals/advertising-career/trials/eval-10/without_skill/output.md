# Paid Media Manager → Head of Growth: 3-Year Plan

🎯 **Goal:** Head of Growth within 3 years, using 240h of study (attribution, incrementality, MMM, forecasting) as the technical spine, applied against your $180K/month budget for visible wins.

## Sequencing logic

Order matters. Each course should build credibility for the next, not just knowledge.

1. **Advanced attribution** (closest to your current job — easiest first win)
2. **Incrementality certification** (challenges attribution, proves causal rigor)
3. **MMM** (aggregate budget allocation — needs the stats maturity from steps 1-2)
4. **Forecasting bootcamp** (capstone — synthesizes measurement into predictive planning, the most "head of growth"-shaped skill, freshest going into your promotion push)

Don't reorder to "easiest first" beyond this — attribution and incrementality are prerequisites for MMM credibility (you'll triangulate MMM outputs against your incrementality test results, which only works if incrementality comes first).

## Year 1: Study phase (Months 1–12, ~240h, ~5h/week average)

| Quarter | Course | Hours | Study focus |
|---|---|---|---|
| Q1 (Mo 1-3) | Attribution | 50h | Multi-touch models, data-driven attribution, dark funnel, B2B multi-stakeholder buying committees, privacy-era tracking |
| Q2 (Mo 4-6) | Incrementality | 60h | Geo holdouts, PSA/ghost ads, conversion lift, synthetic control |
| Q3 (Mo 7-9) | MMM | 70h | Bayesian MMM, adstock/saturation curves, cross-channel optimization, triangulation with incrementality |
| Q4 (Mo 10-12) | Forecasting | 60h | Pipeline/revenue forecasting, scenario planning, RevOps/Finance collaboration |

Rule: don't start a new course until the previous one has produced an applied artifact (below). Theory without application doesn't move you toward Head of Growth.

## Applied deliverables — one per course, each is a visibility event with your director

| Course | Deliverable | Target date |
|---|---|---|
| Attribution | Attribution audit: expose blind spots (dark funnel, offline/sales-assisted conversions), fix tracking gaps | End Mo 3 |
| Incrementality | First test on your largest channel (likely paid search brand or LinkedIn ABM). Quantify incremental CAC vs. attributed CAC | End Mo 6 |
| MMM | Lightweight MMM on 12-24 months of the $180K/month spend, validated against your incrementality results. Budget reallocation proposal | End Mo 9 |
| Forecasting | Spend → pipeline → revenue model with scenarios (+20%/-20% budget). Feed into annual planning with Finance | End Mo 12 |

Each deliverable should be positioned as strategy, not reporting: "here's $X/month misallocated" beats "here's a dashboard."

## Year 2 (Months 13–24): Expand scope

1. Propose owning full-funnel measurement, not just paid media — natural ask after four consecutive strategic wins.
2. Take ownership of the quarterly budget planning process end-to-end.
3. Mentor a teammate on incrementality testing — signals leadership, not just individual contribution.
4. Turn the four deliverables into portfolio case studies (numbers, before/after, your reasoning).
5. Leverage your existing meetup/conference organizing — speak on MMM or incrementality at your own GenAI/growth events or a marketing one. External visibility compounds internal credibility.

Skill gaps outside these 240h (lifecycle/retention marketing, product-led growth, team management) — don't burn course budget on them. Cover with books/podcasts/shadowing during Year 2, informally.

## Year 3 (Months 25–36): Promotion push

1. Push internally for a Head of Growth / Growth Lead title, using the scope expansion from Year 2 as evidence.
2. Run an external search in parallel — don't wait passively on one internal decision.
3. Target Series B-D B2B SaaS, where Head of Growth typically owns budget + measurement + forecasting — exactly what you'll have built.
4. Interview with the four case studies as your primary proof, not the certificates themselves.

## Checkpoints

- ✅ End of Year 1: 4/4 courses done, 4/4 deliverables shipped and presented.
- ⏳ Month 15: reassess — if scope hasn't expanded despite the wins, start external search earlier than Year 3.
- ✅ End of Year 2: portfolio complete, one external speaking slot done, one direct report or mentee.

## Risk

If work spikes and study time gets squeezed, protect the *order*, not the pace — slipping the schedule is fine, skipping incrementality to jump to MMM is not, since it breaks the triangulation story that makes the MMM deliverable credible.
