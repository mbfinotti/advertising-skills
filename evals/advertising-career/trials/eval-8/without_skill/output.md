## The core problem with the draft

Three of your four points are weak as written:

- **"240+ campcampaigns"** is a volume metric, not an impact metric. On its own it reads as "I was busy," not "I drove results." It needs a number next to it — spend managed, revenue/ROAS influenced, or YoY growth.
- **"Everyone hired the same year as me got promoted"** is a peer-comparison argument, not a merit argument. It can read as entitlement rather than evidence, and it invites the manager to explain why your case is different rather than agree it's the same. Cut it from the pitch itself — save it only as a private fallback if the conversation stalls.
- **"Without any extra pay"** frames the coverage story as a grievance. The fact itself (you absorbed two accounts) is your strongest point — proof you're already doing senior-scope work. Let the facts carry it; don't editorialize about pay in the same breath, or it reads as resentment instead of readiness.
