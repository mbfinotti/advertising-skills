## What to do in the next 3 weeks

This is a menu, ranked by value per hour, not by what's easiest:

- **Value order:** quantify the owned number (spend/budget absorbed, and its movement) → get budget/forecast ownership confirmed in writing → map evidence to the actual Senior JD → pull the market anchor.
- **Effort order:** market anchor (20 min) → mapping to the JD (an hour) → quantifying the owned number (a few hours pulling reports) → getting forecast ownership formalised (needs your manager's sign-off — could stretch past the review date).

Forecast/budget ownership is the item this order starves — highest value, but it's the one you don't fully control on a 3-week clock. Don't wait on it to build the case; do raise it explicitly as a forward ask in the same conversation, even if it isn't resolved before you present. A 3-week deadline promotes "quantify what you already did" over "wait for a title change to become official" — do the first now, ask for the second live.

**Benchmark to change course:** if the review doesn't produce either the promotion or a written commitment to hand you budget/forecast ownership on a dated timeline, that's the signal the senior track is blocked at Bramblewick specifically — not that your case was weak. Set a date 3 months out to revisit whether to push again internally or look elsewhere.
