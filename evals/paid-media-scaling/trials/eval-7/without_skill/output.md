## Why "just turn up the dial" is the actual risk here

Your last-30-day signals are a textbook saturation pattern, not a creative problem:

- **Frequency 3.6 and climbing** + **unique reach down 2 months straight** = you're re-showing the same people ads, not finding new ones. This is happening _at $70K_, before any of the new budget lands.
- **Penetration 37%** confirms the addressable pool at current targeting settings is running out of headroom.
- **CTR flat** is the one good sign — creative isn't fatigued, the audience pool is.
- **CPM +31% YoY** is partly seasonal (Q4 approaching) and partly self-inflicted: rising frequency means you're bidding against your own prior impressions in the same pool.

Doubling spend into a shrinking, already-saturated pool without expanding reach will spike frequency further, keep pushing CPMs up, and is the most likely way to blow through your 2.2 MER floor. Budget and reach have to scale together.

## The ramp: budget + reach expansion + creative, tied together

**Weeks 1–2 ($80.5K → $92.6K): unlock reach before pushing harder**

- Before/alongside the first two bumps, add 1–2 new audience inputs to the same campaign: a fresh lookalike seed (recent purchasers, not the stale seed likely driving current saturation), broadened age/geo, or shift toward Advantage+ audience if not already there.
- Launch new creative at ~1–2/week pace (from your 6/month capacity) targeted at the _new_ audience slices, not just refreshed variants for the same saturated pool.
- Retire the weakest 1–2 of the 9 proven ads if their individual frequency is already elevated — don't just stack new ads on top of a bloated rotation.

**Weeks 3–4 ($106.5K → $122.4K): scale into the expansion, watch frequency**

- Second wave of new creative goes live (cumulative ~4–5 new ads by end of week 4).
- If reach is still declining despite the audience expansion, that's your signal the pool itself is capped — don't push to week 5's bump; hold and add another expansion vector (new interest cluster, additional placement mix, e.g., Reels/Audience Network if underutilized) first.

**Week 5 ($140K, target hit): stress-test, don't relax**

- This is the highest-risk week — biggest cumulative jump (2x baseline) landing on an audience you've now roughly doubled the reach pool for over 4 weeks.
- Full creative complement should be live by now (~9 proven, trimmed as needed, + ~6 new).

**Weeks 6–8 (hold at $140K): consolidate, don't compound**

- No further budget increases. Use these 3 weeks to let the algorithm stabilize at the new spend level, keep cycling the remaining new-creative capacity (final ~6 ads) to replace anything showing frequency fatigue, and rebuild a reach buffer before any further scaling decision post-week 8.
- This also positions you to enter Black Friday/Cyber Monday from a stable base rather than mid-ramp.

## Guardrails — check every Monday before approving the next bump

| Signal       | Threshold                                          | Action                                                                  |
| ------------ | -------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Blended MER  | < 2.2 for 2 consecutive weeks                      | Freeze budget, hold at current level                                    |
| Frequency    | > 4.5–5                                            | Pause that week's bump, prioritize reach expansion before next increase |
| Unique reach | Still declining after audience expansion           | Do not proceed to next budget tier; add another expansion lever first   |
| New-ad CPA   | > 20% worse than proven-ad average for a full week | Pause that ad, don't let it eat budget share                            |

## Creative cadence (6/month → 12 over 8 weeks)

- Don't just add 12 ads on top of the existing 9 — that fragments spend and reach diminishing returns hits per-ad too.
- Target a steady-state live rotation of ~10–12 ads: as new ones prove out, sunset the proven ads with the highest individual frequency, not the oldest by calendar.
- Sequence new ads to arrive _with_ audience expansion (weeks 1–4), not after — new creative into a saturated pool won't fix a reach problem, only new creative into new reach will.

**Bottom line:** the 15%/week pace gets you to $140K in 5 weeks, not 8 — use the extra 3 as a hold/consolidation phase. But the bigger fix isn't pacing, it's that every budget bump needs a matching reach-expansion action; without one, you're pouring more money into a pool that's already visibly running out of new people.
