## Sanity check: don't jump to $80K

**The blended MER is the wrong number to justify this.** It's averaging in retention/email/branded spend that has nothing to do with prospecting's marginal economics. The number that matters here is what the *next dollar* of prospecting spend returns — and that number is deteriorating fast, hidden underneath a still-healthy average.

### What the trend actually shows

| Month | Spend | Revenue | Cumulative MER | **Marginal MER (this month's added $ vs last)** |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| June | $40K | $122K | 3.05 | — |
| July | $48K | $140K | 2.92 | (140-122)/(48-40) = **2.25** |
| August | $56K | $152K | 2.71 | (152-140)/(56-48) = **1.50** |

Cumulative MER looks fine (3.05 → 2.92 → 2.71, still well above the 2.0 break-even). But the marginal MER — the return on each incremental $8K — has collapsed from 2.25 to 1.50 in one month. **1.50 is already below your 2.0 break-even.** August's last $8K of spend lost money on a pure MER basis; it's just being masked by the profitable spend that came before it.

### Why this matters for the $80K jump

- If that decay rate holds (marginal MER dropping ~0.75 per $8K step), the *next* $8K increment (56K→64K) would land close to marginal MER ~0.75, and the increment after that (64K→72K) near zero or negative. A jump straight to $80K adds $24K — three of those steps at once — almost entirely in territory where the incremental spend is very plausibly unprofitable, not just "less efficient."
- You'd only see this in the topline blended number weeks later, after the spend is committed and creative/audience is already fatigued.
- $40K→$80K is a 100% increase in a single month vs. the ~15-20% steps you've been taking. That's not a continuation of the trend you tested — it's an untested regime. Prospecting audiences and creative supply don't scale linearly; this pattern (revenue decelerating as spend rises) is classic audience saturation, and doubling spend in one jump is likely to hit it harder, not proportionally.

### Recommendation

⚠️ **Don't approve $80K.** Go-ahead only for an incremental step consistent with the trend you've actually tested — e.g. **$64-65K** (another ~$8-9K step), not $80K.

Before committing even that: pull CPM/CPA and frequency trends for August to confirm this is saturation (audience/creative fatigue) rather than a data blip, since that determines whether new creative can reset the curve or whether $56K is close to this channel's real ceiling for now.

If you do want $80K eventually, get there by watching **marginal MER weekly** as you step up (not blended, not monthly cumulative) — pull back the moment a weekly increment's marginal MER drops under ~2.5-3.0 (leaving margin above the 2.0 floor), rather than after the month closes and the blended number tells you too late.
