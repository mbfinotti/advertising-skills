# Scaling Brontide Labs Paid Media: $25K → $75K/mo in 90 Days

Before the phased plan: four structural risks in what you've described make a straight-line 3x untested. Address these in parallel with scaling, not after.

## Risks to flag before spending more

- **Zero elasticity data.** Budgets never moved since March. You have no evidence for how CPL/CAC respond to a budget increase on this account — 3x is a leap, not an extrapolation.
- **Creative supply won't support 3x spend.** 4 winning ads + 2 new/month is a cadence built for $25K. At $75K, narrow B2B audiences (especially LinkedIn) will hit frequency fatigue fast. You need creative throughput to scale with spend, not stay flat.
- **Cash conversion mismatch.** You pay ad accounts weekly by card; customers pay net-60, on top of whatever the webinar-lead-to-close sales cycle already adds. CFO approved the _media budget_ — confirm they also modeled the _working capital gap_: 60-90+ days of spend committed before any of it converts to cash. At $75K/mo weekly card billing (~$17K/week), also check the card's credit limit.
- **Tracking is 12/15, not 15/15.** Scaling amplifies whatever the missing 3 points are (commonly: offline/closed-revenue import, cross-platform dedup, MQL→SQL loop). Fix these before the spend increase makes the blind spots 3x more expensive.

## Step plan

**1. Week 1 — close the measurement and finance gaps**

- Get the tracking audit's 3 missing points itemized and fixed or explicitly accepted as known blind spots.
- Model cash conversion cycle (spend → lead → close → invoice → net-60 cash-in) with the CFO. Confirm working capital and card limit cover the gap, not just the ad spend line.

**2. Week 1-2 — run a controlled elasticity test**

- Increase daily budget 20-25% (standard threshold to avoid resetting delivery/learning phase on Meta and LinkedIn).
- Hold 2 weeks. Track CPL, CAC, and lead quality (not just volume) by platform and by ad.
- Only proceed to the next step-up if marginal CPL stays within your target payback threshold.

**3. Ongoing — fix the creative bottleneck before fixing the budget**

- 2 ads/month was sized for $25K. Target 3-4x that cadence before spend reaches 2x current level.
- Add a second freelancer or in-house resource in parallel, don't wait for month 3 to feel the fatigue.
- Track frequency per ad per platform; retire/refresh anything crossing your fatigue threshold, not on a fixed calendar.

**4. Month 1 — ramp in 20-25% steps, not one jump**

- Target trajectory: ~$25K → ~$35-40K by end of month 1, gated by step 2's results at each increment.
- Split incremental budget across platforms deliberately — don't just pour it into whichever already spends fastest.

**5. Month 2 — widen audience before pushing more into the same one**

- Layer in new segments (lookalikes, retargeting, adjacent job titles/industries) so incremental budget isn't chasing the same saturated LinkedIn audience.
- Target ~$40K → ~$55-60K, same 20-25% step discipline, contingent on CPL holding.

**6. Month 3 — final push to $75K, with a pre-agreed kill line**

- Only take the last step if CAC/CPL through month 2 stayed within the threshold the CFO underwrote — not "decent," a number.
- Agree in advance what makes you pause or roll back a step (e.g., CPL rises >X% for 2 consecutive weeks) — decide this now, not mid-ramp.

**7. Throughout — report marginal, not blended, numbers**

- Track marginal CAC/CPL on each incremental dollar, separated by platform. Blended averages will hide a platform or segment going underwater while another compensates.

## Bottom line

The budget was approved; the operating capacity to deploy it wasn't yet demonstrated. The gating factor isn't the CFO's sign-off — it's creative velocity, audience depth, and cash timing. Sequence those three ahead of the dollars, and let each budget step earn the next one rather than committing to $75K on a calendar.
