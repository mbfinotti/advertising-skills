## The tension, stated once

The skill this list is built from audited ~50 paid-advertising sources and found: tool-vendor blogs are the freshest medium in this field, official platform release notes are the only reliable notice of feature/policy changes, and _every_ independent-sounding expert (Foxwell, Loomer, Nik Sharma, Taylor Holiday, Savannah Sanchez, Jyll Saskin Gales, AJ Wilcox — the entire DTC Meta/TikTok specialist ecosystem) monetizes through an agency, a course, or a paid community. Cutting all four categories — tool vendor, agency, course seller, platform — doesn't trim that list. It removes essentially the entire specialist tactical layer for Meta and TikTok, leaving general trade journalism as the only category left standing.

Practical effect: this reading list can tell your team _that_ Meta or TikTok changed something, days later, via a reporter — not give them platform-native release notes the day it ships, and not give them a DTC operator's opinion on how to react. I built it to the letter of the requirement anyway. Flagging it once; not re-litigating it below.
