# Lakewell Outdoor — Paid Social Team Reading List

**Budget:** 60 min/week. **Filter applied:** no ad-tool vendors, no agencies, no course sellers, no Meta/TikTok own channels. Everything below is trade journalism, academic research, or independent commentary with no product, course, or ad-management service to sell.

## Weekly rotation (pick ~60 min total)

**1. Modern Retail — 15 min**
DTC- and e-commerce-focused trade publication (part of Digiday Media, ad-supported). Closest direct match to Lakewell's world: covers how DTC brands are actually spending on Meta/TikTok, what's working, what's breaking. Skim the newsletter, open 1–2 stories.

**2. Digiday — 15 min**
Independent ad-industry trade press, subscription + ad-supported. Reports critically on platform changes (Meta's Advantage+ shifts, TikTok algorithm/policy changes, iOS/attribution fallout) without a stake in any tool or platform's success. Read the Marketing or Platforms vertical.

**3. AdExchanger — 15 min**
Ad-tech trade journalism, subscription-supported. More technical than Digiday — good for understanding _why_ Meta/TikTok bidding, measurement, and privacy changes happen, written by reporters, not by anyone selling a fix for them.

**4. Marketing Brew — 10–15 min**
Free daily/weekly digest (Morning Brew, ad-supported, editorially independent). Fastest way to stay current if a week gets tight — treat as the fallback that always fits the hour.

## Monthly/occasional deep read (rotate in when the weekly news is quiet)

**Ehrenberg-Bass Institute (marketingscience.info)** — University of South Australia's marketing science research center. Free published research on what actually drives advertising effectiveness (the _How Brands Grow_ body of work). No commercial ties to any platform or agency — the most rigorously independent source on this list. Not weekly-cadence content, but worth a slot once a month; it's the best counterweight to whatever platform-reported "best practices" surface elsewhere.

**WARC** — global marketing research body, subscription-funded, independent editorial. Publishes cross-platform benchmark and effectiveness studies. Flag for the CMO as a possible paid add if budget allows; skip if not — everything above is free.

## Explicitly excluded, and why

- **Tool vendors** (Triple Whale, Northbeam, Motion, Madgicx, Revealbot, Varos, etc.) — blogs exist to sell the platform's own dashboard/attribution story.
- **Agencies** (Common Thread Collective, Tier 11, Foxwell Digital, Consumer Acquisition, etc.) — content is a funnel into retainer sales.
- **Course sellers** (Ezra Firestone, Depesh Mandalia, Ben Heath, Andrew Foxwell's paid programs, etc.) — free content is a lead magnet for paid training.
- **Meta/TikTok own channels** (Meta for Business blog, TikTok for Business blog, newsroom posts) — self-reporting on their own ad products.
- Borderline calls I left out rather than flag as "independent": Eric Seufert (Mobile Dev Memo) and Antonio García Martínez both write sharp, technically deep commentary on the ad ecosystem, but both run paid consulting/advisory practices adjacent to what they cover — didn't meet the CMO's "no ambiguity" bar, so excluded rather than caveated in.

## Suggested cadence for the team

- Assign no single-source ownership — rotate who reads what each week and drops a 3-bullet summary in Slack, so the collective hour covers all four weekly sources across the team rather than one person reading everything.
- Slot the Ehrenberg-Bass/WARC deep read into whoever's week is lightest on campaign fires.
