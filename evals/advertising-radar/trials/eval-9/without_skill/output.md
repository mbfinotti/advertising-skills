# Parcelbird Growth Radar

A standing weekly system to stay current on LinkedIn, Meta, and Google paid media — for both the B2B logistics SaaS and the consumer label-printer — in 90 minutes, favoring breadth over depth.

**Legend:** 🟢 independent · 🟠 vendor/agency-run (useful, but has skin in the game) · 🔴 clearly conflicted (sells what it promotes) — kept for signal, read with that lens.

## The weekly 90-minute routine

1. **15 min — Tier 1.** Scan platform change logs. This is where policy/algorithm changes land first.
2. **30 min — Tier 2.** Drop into **one or two** live communities (rotate weekly). Skim unread, don't reply unless something's directly actionable.
3. **45 min — Tier 3.** Skim subject lines/headlines across the newsletter list below. Deep-read only what touches an active campaign. Rotate which sub-group gets the deeper read each week (B2B one week, DTC the next).

Do this same slot every week — habit over intensity.

## One-time setup (~20 min)

- **Bold action:** create a "Parcelbird Radar" folder in an RSS reader (Feedly or similar) and subscribe to every Tier 1 and Tier 3 blog/RSS feed.
- **Bold action:** sign up for every newsletter above with a dedicated inbox filter/label so they don't compete with regular mail.
- **Bold action:** join the Slack/Discord/Facebook Group communities in Tier 2; mute notifications, visit on your schedule instead.
- **Bold action:** build an X/Twitter list called "Parcelbird PPC" seeded from active #ppcchat participants, for quick scanning.

## Maintenance

Every quarter, spend 15 minutes pruning: drop anything that's gone quiet, add anything you've heard three practitioners reference independently. Keep the conflict flags — don't drop a source just because it's biased, drop it only if it's gone stale or low-signal.
