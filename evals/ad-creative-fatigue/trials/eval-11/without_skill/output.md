## Verdict: ❌ Don't green-light the emergency creative sprint — the data doesn't support the "ad wore out" diagnosis.

### Why creative fatigue doesn't fit
Creative wear-out shows up as **declining engagement**: CTR drops first (audience tunes out), often followed by CVR decay as lower-intent viewers keep clicking. Here:
- Link CTR: 1.35% vs 1.33% baseline → flat to slightly **up**
- CVR: flat

If the creative were fatigued, CTR would be the first metric to break down. It hasn't moved. The funnel from impression → click → conversion is intact. That rules out audience wear-out as the primary driver.

### What the numbers actually say
CPA is a function of CPM, CTR, and CVR:
> CPA ≈ CPM ÷ (CTR × CVR)

- CPM up 24%, CTR flat, CVR flat → CPA should rise ~24% from CPM alone.
- Observed CPA is up 28% — close enough that CPM inflation explains almost all of it. No hidden funnel breakage needed to account for the gap.

### Most likely real cause: auction cost inflation, not creative decay
Mid-November CPM spikes are a well-known seasonal pattern — every retailer (jewelry included) ramps prospecting budgets ahead of Black Friday, auction competition intensifies platform-wide, and CPMs climb for **everyone**, regardless of creative quality. A 9-week-old video isn't unusually old; the timing (three weeks of rising CPM landing right before BF) matches the seasonal auction curve far better than a fatigue curve.

### Before touching creative, confirm with a quick diagnostic (1-2 days, not 2 weeks)
- 🎯 **Frequency**: pull frequency for this ad specifically. Rising frequency + flat CTR = fatigue is *not yet* happening; frequency wouldn't even be the driver here since CTR is stable.
- 🎯 **Account-wide CPM**: check CPM trend on other active campaigns/ads. If they're rising too, it's platform/seasonal, not this creative.
- 🎯 **Placement mix**: check if Advantage+ shifted delivery toward pricier placements (e.g., more Reels/Stories, less Feed) — that alone can push CPM up without touching CTR/CVR.
- 🎯 **Bid strategy/budget changes**: any recent budget increase or cost cap change that could've pushed delivery into a more expensive auction segment.
- 🎯 **Year-over-year**: compare to this account's Nov CPM curve last year, if available — this pattern likely repeats annually.

### Recommended action instead
- ✅ Keep the current creative live — it's still converting efficiently on a per-click basis.
- ✅ Protect efficiency via bid strategy (cost cap/bid cap adjustment) or audience expansion to counter rising CPMs, rather than a creative swap.
- ✅ If you want new creative for Black Friday, prepare it as **planned iteration for the BF push itself**, not a 2-week emergency sprint landing right before your highest-stakes week.

### Risk of doing the sprint anyway
Launching unproven creative right before Black Friday means eating Meta's learning-phase cost spike (typically worse than a 24-28% CPM/CPA bump) at the exact moment you need stable, predictable performance. That's a bigger risk than the problem you're trying to solve.
