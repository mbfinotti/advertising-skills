## The numbers don't support fatigue

- **CTR is up**, not down: 1.24% this week vs 1.21% trailing month. Creative fatigue shows up as declining CTR as the same eyeballs get bored — that's not happening here.
- **CVR is down 0.1pt** (3.1% vs 3.2%) — small, and could easily be noise, seasonality, landing page, offer, or audience-quality shifts rather than the ad itself.
- **CPA +4%** is almost fully explained by the CVR dip alone (a CVR drop of ~3% combined with normal CPM/auction pressure gets you to ~4% CPA increase without any help from frequency).

Frequency 3.0 is a rough heuristic, not a law — it varies a lot by audience size, campaign objective, and creative format. A rising CTR at 3.4 frequency is a fairly strong signal that this specific creative hasn't worn out.

## What's more likely going on

- Trailing-month CVR includes weeks you may have had a better mix of audiences, promos, or seasonality — comparing this week's CVR to a blended trailing average can manufacture an apparent "decline" that isn't creative-driven.
- CPA upward pressure across cold prospecting in skincare/DTC right now could just as easily be auction/CPM inflation (check your CPM trend — if CPM is also up, that alone explains most of the CPA move and has nothing to do with this creative).

## What I'd actually do

1. **Don't hard-swap tomorrow.** Killing a creative with rising CTR on a frequency threshold alone risks discarding a still-working ad and resetting the algorithm's learning phase for no real gain.
2. **Check CPM trend this week vs trailing month first.** If CPM is up ~4%+, that's your CPA answer, not fatigue — and no creative swap fixes that.
3. **Launch the new video as a second ad in the same ad set (or a duplicated ad set), not a replacement.** Let both run and let Meta split traffic. This costs you nothing and de-risks the decision — you're not betting the whole budget on the new creative being better.
4. **Set a real trigger for pausing the old one**: CTR actually turning down week-over-week, or the new creative beating it on CPA/CVR with enough spend/conversions to be statistically meaningful (don't call it on <50 conversions per variant).
5. **Watch CVR for one more week** before treating it as a trend — one week of -0.1pt is within normal noise for most DTC accounts unless your conversion volume is very high.

**Bottom line:** the creative isn't the problem by the data you have — CTR is your fatigue canary and it's healthy. Introduce the new video as a challenger alongside the current one rather than a same-day swap, and let CPM/CVR data (not the frequency number) decide who gets paused.
