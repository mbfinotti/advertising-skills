## Verdict: Run 1, hold 1, skip 1

**✅ Thought-leader ads (CEO posts, Book a Demo CTA) — primary format, ~75% of budget**
Best fit for cold, guarded compliance buyers. Credibility from a named executive lands better than brand-voice ads with this audience, CPMs are typically lower than Message Ads, and it doubles as retargeting-pool growth (your 210 is too thin to build a strategy on). Split the CTA: most impressions on "engage/visit site," a smaller slice on hard "Book a demo" — cold compliance leaders rarely convert to a call on first touch, and over-indexing on a hard CTA here just inflates CPL on unqualified clicks.

**⚠️ Sponsored inbox/Message Ads — hold, don't run cold**
Cold-DMing a demo pitch to compliance officers is a bad match: this exact audience is trained to distrust unsolicited outreach and to flag it. Deliverability/spam-complaint risk is real and it can taint sender reputation for future touches. Don't cut it entirely — reserve it for your retargeting pool once it's grown past ~1,000 (LinkedIn Matched Audiences won't even activate reliably at 210), where a message to someone who already visited the site reads as relevant, not cold.

**❌ Connected TV — skip for now**
$9K/month split three ways leaves nothing meaningful for CTV, which historically needs a much higher spend floor to get real delivery. You also can't target "compliance leaders in NL" precisely on CTV — you'd be buying broad reach with no attribution path back to a niche B2B title. It's a brand-credibility play for a later stage with 3-5x this budget, not a Q4 test.

## Suggested $9K/month split
- **Thought-leader ads: ~$6,750** — weight by market size, e.g. DE $3,000 / FR $2,250 / NL $1,500
- **Message Ads: ~$2,250**, restricted to warm segments (site retargeting pool + engaged post viewers), not cold prospecting lists
- **CTV: $0** — revisit once the retargeting pool and budget both scale

## Why the pool size matters here
210 members is below LinkedIn's practical activation threshold for Matched Audiences. Treat pool-building as the Q4 priority — thought-leader ads are your growth engine for it — before leaning on any format that depends on a warm audience to work.
