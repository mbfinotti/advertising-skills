**Fit-row order (post-substitution):** single image / document ad with CTA (demo capture) > Thought Leader Ads, no CTA (credibility) — everything else in the original plan fails a hard gate outright, so there's no efficiency race to rank between rows 1–3 as submitted. **What moved this:** the EU consent gate deleted inbox/message outright (question 10's answer, given); the no-CTA rule on Thought Leader Ads split format 2 into two units instead of reordering it; the $9K budget, well under CTV's $15K/month floor, deleted CTV rather than demoting it.

**Effort:** the two substitutes (single image, document ad) sit near the bottom of the effort axis — near-zero asset work, no shoot. CTV sits at the top — a video asset plus a standing production job — which is a second reason it's a poor match for a $9K/month program on top of the spend floor.

**Compliance cost:** the format you asked for first (inbox/message) sits at the *top* of the compliance-cost axis for the entire skill — opt-in consent regime, legal sign-off before a single send — which is exactly why it's the one that fails outright rather than merely underperforming.

## 3. Launch-blocker list

- **[blocker]** Sponsored inbox messages to cold DE/FR/NL: consent gate fails. Do not launch as designed. Fix: swap to a feed format (above).
- **[blocker]** Thought Leader Ads with a "Book a demo" button: format carries no CTA on image/video. Do not launch as designed. Fix: split into an awareness unit (no CTA) and a separate feed ad carrying the CTA.
- **Not currently a blocker, but recommend against:** Connected TV at $9K/month total — below the ~$15K/month floor where CTV is worth funding on this platform. Confirm DE/FR/NL are supported CTV geographies before treating this as anything but dropped.

Zero blockers is the launch threshold — as submitted, this plan has two. Both are fixable with the substitutes above, not with better creative or tighter targeting.

## 4. Post-launch watch list

1. **Frequency out of band.** DE/FR/NL compliance leaders is a narrow cold segment. Watch frequency against the ~1.0–2.5 cold-prospecting band; a fast climb points at an undersized audience for the budget, not weak creative.
2. **Placement/unit-breakdown divergence.** The awareness Thought Leader unit and the demo-CTA feed ad are doing two different jobs — don't average their numbers. Break results down by unit before concluding either "works" or "doesn't."
3. **Hook/hold rate on the demo-CTA creative.** Once CTV is dropped, the credibility job sits entirely on the Thought Leader unit — watch its stop/stay rates specifically so a weak opening isn't mistaken for a format problem.

**Confirmation step:** start with the placement/format breakdown already in Campaign Manager reporting — free, minutes. Escalate to an isolated ad set only if it stays ambiguous. A holdout isn't warranted yet — $9K/month is too small a program to sacrifice reach on one.

**Separately:** your 210-member retargeting pool sits below LinkedIn's 300-member campaign minimum. It isn't one of the three formats in question, but it can't run as its own campaign yet either — worth knowing before you plan a warm layer for Q1.
