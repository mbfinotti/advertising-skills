# Anniversary Sale Video Format Plan — 10-Day Sprint

## Capacity reality check

10 days, 2 new assets/month capacity ≈ 0.6 new assets in this window. You cannot shoot new UGC video from scratch and edit it well in this timeframe as a one-person team. Budget for this sprint:

- 1 new asset you actually produce/edit yourself
- 1–2 UGC videos you **brief and outsource** (production isn't your time — briefing is)
- Everything else = repurposing the brand film + product statics

## Format matrix by placement

| Placement                        | Spec                                                                    | Best source                                   |
| -------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------- |
| Reels/Stories (cold scroll)      | 9:16, full-bleed, 3–10s hook baked into first frame, captions burned in | UGC or templated statics — not the brand film |
| Feed (in-app, sound-off default) | 4:5 or 1:1, captions mandatory                                          | Brand film cutdowns work here                 |
| Stories (retargeting/warm)       | 9:16, can be more polished/branded                                      | Brand film cutdowns work here too             |
| Reels (retargeting/warm)         | 9:16, still needs a hook but can be product-forward not UGC             | Brand film cutdowns or statics-to-video       |

The split that matters: **cold traffic wants native/UGC-feel, warm traffic (your followers, past customers, retargeting) tolerates and often prefers polished brand footage.** Your anniversary sale audience is mostly warm — don't over-index on UGC just because it's trendy.

## What to do with the existing brand film

Dialogue-driven 16:9 doesn't survive a straight center-crop to 9:16 — you'll lose framing and the dialogue does nothing sound-off. Don't force it into Reels cold-scroll slots.

Instead:

1. Pull 3–5 strongest visual beats (product in use, texture, hero shots) — no dialogue needed.
2. Re-edit as silent, caption-led cutdowns: text does the selling, footage is texture/proof.
3. Export at 4:5 for feed and 9:16 with blurred/extended background (not crop) for Stories.
4. Use these for warm placements: retargeting Stories, feed posts, sale countdown Stories.

This gets you 3–5 "new" assets in an afternoon of editing, zero new shooting.

## UGC that fits a 1-person team

Don't shoot it yourself. Brief it out:

- Platforms: Billo, Insense, JoinBrands, or Backdoor — pay per video ($150–400/video), turnaround 3–7 days, fits your 10-day window if ordered today.
- Brief: 1 core script (problem → unboxing/use → reaction → offer), 15–30s, ask for 2 hook variants per creator so you get testable variants for free.
- Order 2 creators minimum — one video isn't enough to know if the format works, and creator variance is high.
- These count as "outsourced," not against your 2-assets/month personal capacity.

If budget is zero: use your product statics + a simple template (CapCut/VN) — handheld-feel text overlays, trending audio, jump cuts — to fake the UGC pattern-interrupt without hiring anyone. It won't be as good as real UGC but it's fast and cheap.

## 10-day build order

1. **Day 1–2**: Order 2 UGC videos from a marketplace (this is your longest lead time — start it first).
2. **Day 1–3**: Cut 3–5 caption-led clips from the brand film (4:5 + 9:16 versions).
3. **Day 3–5**: Template 2–3 product statics into short vertical videos (text-overlay sale hooks) for Reels cold traffic while UGC is in production.
4. **Day 7–8**: UGC videos land — trim to 15–20s, add captions, cut a 2nd hook variant from each if the creator didn't provide one.
5. **Day 9**: Assemble placement map (below) and load into ad/organic scheduler.
6. **Day 10+**: Launch. Watch 3-second hook retention on the UGC/statics pieces first — that's your read on whether format bet paid off.

## Placement map for launch

- **Reels/Stories, cold/prospecting**: UGC videos + templated statics (hook-led, sound-off safe)
- **Reels/Stories, retargeting/warm**: brand film cutdowns (caption-led)
- **Feed, all audiences**: brand film cutdowns, 4:5
- **Feed, cold prospecting**: whichever UGC/statics variant has best early hook retention, repurposed to 1:1

Don't build all 4 rows from scratch — 5 core assets (2 UGC + 3 brand-film cutdowns), each exported in 2 aspect ratios, covers the matrix.
