## Verdict: Reject all three recommendations as written.

Each one takes a generic industry stat and applies it without checking it against Vantorra's actual numbers or funnel logic. Here's the breakdown.

### (a) Move demo bookings from landing page to in-platform lead forms — **Reject**

- The deck cites landing pages at 4%. Ours converts at **6.5%** — already 62% above their benchmark. The premise doesn't apply to us.
- The comparison is apples-to-oranges: a lead form submit and a **booked calendar slot** are different outcomes. A form submit still needs a follow-up email/call to become a meeting, with its own drop-off (often 30-50% loss). Our 6.5% is already _booked meetings_, not raw form fills — comparing it to a form-fill rate understates our real conversion.
- Action: don't touch it. If anything, test adding an in-platform form as a _secondary_ path for logged-in trial users, not a replacement for the calendar on the landing page.

### (b) 6-second bumper ads for demo requests — **Reject**

- "Human attention span is 8 seconds, shorter than a goldfish" is a zombie stat — it traces back to a 2015 Microsoft/Statistic Brain report with no verifiable source, and has been debunked repeatedly. It's not a real basis for a media-buy decision.
- Even ignoring the stat: bumpers are a reach/frequency format (unskippable, forced completion) built for brand recall, not direct response. B2B legal-tech is a considered purchase — buyers need to understand what "contract review SaaS" does and trust it before requesting a demo. Six seconds can't carry that.
- Action: don't allocate to bumpers for demo-gen. If there's appetite for short-form video, use it for upper-funnel retargeting/awareness, measured on brand lift or view-through — not tied to demo request volume.

### (c) Shift most budget to carousels for lower cost per conversion — **Reject as framed**

- You said budget isn't the constraint. That means the optimization target should be **total qualified pipeline**, not CPA. Chasing a cheaper cost-per-conversion when you're not budget-limited is optimizing the wrong variable.
- A 30-50% lower CPA is also a red flag for lead quality, not a pure win — cheaper conversions on a carousel unit often come from lower-intent clicks. Nothing in the deck says whether that stat controls for close rate or downstream pipeline value.
- Action: don't shift "most" budget. Run carousels as an incremental test alongside existing formats, and judge them on show-up rate / SQL rate / pipeline generated — not on cost per form-fill.

## What to actually do

1. Keep the landing page + embedded calendar as the primary demo path — it's outperforming the benchmark the deck itself cites.
2. Ignore the bumper ad recommendation entirely; the underlying stat is fabricated and the format doesn't match a considered B2B sale.
3. Test carousels incrementally, funded from incremental budget (since it's not constrained), and kill or scale based on pipeline quality, not CPA.
4. Push back on the deck: the analysis compares Vantorra to generic external benchmarks instead of Vantorra's own funnel data, which is the core methodological problem across all three recommendations.
