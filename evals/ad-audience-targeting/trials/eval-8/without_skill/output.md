# Tallgrass Analytics — 5-Week Pipeline Sprint

## The real constraint

20 hours total (4h/week × 5 weeks), one person, no legal review, hard deadline. This isn't a demand-gen program — it's a triage exercise. Every hour goes to the highest-intent, lowest-risk, fastest-to-close audience first. Cut anything that can't show a board-readable number (meetings booked, opportunities created) inside 5 weeks.

---

## Tier plan

### 🎯 Tier 0 — CRM ∩ Signal lists (runs first)
- **Who:** The subset of your 3,100 CRM contacts whose company also appears in either the 45-company funding/M&A list or the 28-company hiring-signal list.
- **Why first:** Known relationship + live buying trigger = warmest combination you have. Compliance risk is near-zero — you already have a business relationship with these contacts.
- **Channel:** 1:1 personal email/LinkedIn, referencing the specific trigger ("saw the Series B" / "saw the supply-chain analyst req").
- **Effort:** High per-contact, but the list is small (expect 15-35 accounts after dedupe). Budget ~6-8 hours across the sprint.
- **Cost:** $0.
- **Board metric:** Meetings booked, opportunities created — your first and best pipeline number.

### Tier 1 — CRM reactivation (rest of the list)
- **Who:** Remaining ~3,065 CRM contacts, segmented: active customers, touched-in-12mo prospects, stale/cold.
- **Why:** Second-warmest audience, lowest compliance risk (existing relationship or prior opt-in), fastest to execute at volume.
- **Channel:** Segmented email campaign via your CRM's own send tool. Customers → expansion/referral ask tied to the funding news. Prospects → re-engagement tied to relevant signal-list logic ("chains like yours are hiring for this problem right now").
- **Effort:** Low per-contact (batch), 2-3 hours to build and launch.
- **Cost:** $0-500 (deliverability/tooling if not already covered).
- **Board metric:** Reply rate, meetings booked, pipeline $ from existing relationships.

### Tier 2 — Signal-list ABM (non-overlap)
- **Who:** The remaining ~40-55 companies from the two signal lists not already in Tier 0.
- **Why:** No existing relationship, but strong timing signal. Needs real personalization, so it's expensive in hours — ration it.
- **Channel:** Hand-picked top 15-20 accounts (favor the M&A/funding list — cash in hand beats a single job req as a buying signal) get 1:1 outreach + a small direct-mail/gift touch. The rest get a lighter templated-but-personalized sequence.
- **Effort:** ~4-5 hours.
- **Cost:** ~$1,500 (direct mail/gifting for the top 15-20).
- **Board metric:** Meetings booked from cold-but-triggered outreach.

### Tier 3 — Site traffic reveal + retarget
- **Who:** Company-level identification of your 25k monthly visits, filtered to mid-size grocery-chain ICP.
- **Why:** Passive asset you're not using. Contributes volume and a "we have real top-of-funnel interest" chart for the board, not fast 1:1 pipeline.
- **Channel:** A company-level (not person-level) visitor-ID tool → filtered account list → LinkedIn/programmatic ABM retargeting ads.
- **Effort:** ~2 hours setup, then automated.
- **Cost:** ~$1,000/mo tool + ~$8,000 ad spend.
- **Board metric:** Identified ICP accounts visiting, ad-influenced pipeline (directional, not precise — say so to the board).

### Tier 4 — Broad paid demand gen: **cut for this sprint**
Not enough runway for cold paid awareness to mature into board-visible pipeline in 5 weeks with a niche ICP. Revisit after the funding announcement.

## 5-week execution (4h/week = 20h total)

**Week 1 (4h):** ⚠️ Compliance guardrails first (below, ~1h) → dedupe CRM against both signal lists (~1h) → launch Tier 0 outreach to the overlap set (~2h).

**Week 2 (4h):** Finish Tier 0 follow-ups (~1h) → build and send Tier 1 segmented CRM email (~2h) → set up Tier 3 visitor-ID tool + pixel (~1h).

**Week 3 (4h):** Tier 2 — hand-pick top 15-20 signal accounts, send personalized outreach + trigger direct mail (~3h) → check Tier 3 identified-account list, launch retargeting ads (~1h).

**Week 4 (4h):** Follow up all open Tier 0-2 threads, book meetings (~3h) → monitor Tier 3 ad performance (~1h).

**Week 5 (4h):** Final follow-ups, close what's closeable before the announcement (~2h) → build the one-page board pipeline summary (~2h).

## Compliance guardrails (no legal support — do these yourself, don't skip)

- ✅ Every email: real company name/address + working unsubscribe, honored within 24h (not the CAN-SPAM 10-day max — margin for error since nobody's reviewing this).
- ✅ B2B, business-email-only outreach. No personal cell numbers, no SMS/text — skip that channel entirely this sprint (TCPA risk isn't worth it without review).
- ✅ Signal lists built from public sources (funding announcements, public job postings) — that's monitoring, not scraping personal data. Fine as-is.
- ✅ Visitor-ID tool: use company-level identification only, never person-level de-anonymization — several of those tools are under active litigation in the US; don't touch that tier of product.
- ⚠️ Check both signal lists and the CRM for any EU-headquartered chains. If any exist, exclude them from cold outreach — GDPR exposure isn't worth it without someone to review consent basis.
- ✅ Keep a single suppression list, check every send against it.

## What runs first

**Tier 0 — the CRM contacts whose company is also on one of your two signal lists.** Smallest list, warmest relationship, live buying trigger, zero cost, zero real compliance risk. Send those first, this week, before anything else — including before setting up the visitor-ID tool. That's the pipeline number the board sees first, and it costs you nothing but time you already have.
