## 3. Sequencing Notes

- **Champion and End user** surface first — problem identification stage — where they already engage well per your account of the deals.
- **Economic buyer** needs to be reached *directly by paid media*, not only via the champion forwarding a deck — that's the specific fix for "champion can't mobilize the group." Business-case content should hit their feed independent of whether the champion ever sends it.
- **Gatekeeper (Security) should be reached at supplier-selection, not validation.** Reaching them before they're forced into the deal late is the cheapest insurance in this whole map — a security reviewer added late reopens requirements and resets already-agreed work, which is a plausible contributor to your stalls.
- **Weight every Economic Buyer and Gatekeeper angle toward risk reduction, not urgency.** 40–60% of B2B deals end in no-decision, and the majority of those are indecision (fear of a bad call), not preference for the status quo. A "book a demo now" push aimed at a cold gatekeeper reads as pressure, not reassurance — this is the concrete cost of the CEO's blanket-CTA mandate on this specific role.
- Reminder for anyone briefed off this map: **fewer than 30% of eventual buyers ever fill a form on the winning vendor's site**, and ~70% of the buying journey completes before seller contact. Coverage cannot be gated on demo-form fills for the cold roles — that's the other half of the case against a demo-only CTA strategy.
