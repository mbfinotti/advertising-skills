**The core issue: seed quality matters more than seed size past the minimum**

Meta's own algorithm builds a lookalike by finding the shared statistical DNA of your seed. Dilute the seed with low-intent people, and you dilute the DNA it learns from — you don't get "850 good + 23,150 neutral," you get an audience that mostly resembles newsletter subscribers and email openers, not dog-gear buyers.

- 850 purchasers = your strongest possible signal (real money spent on your exact product).
- 24,000 newsletter subscribers = mixed intent — most never bought.
- "Opened a marketing email in 12 months" = the weakest signal in the plan, and largely not real. Since Apple's Mail Privacy Protection (iOS 15, Sept 2021), Apple Mail pre-fetches every image on delivery regardless of whether a human opens the email — so most "opens" in an Apple Mail-heavy list are automated, not behavioral. You'd be seeding on noise.

Merge all three and your 850 real buyers become ~3% of a 25,000+ seed. The lookalike model will optimize for "newsletter/inbox behavior," not "premium dog-gear purchase behavior."

**What actually clears Meta's real bar**

Meta's Lookalike minimum is 100 people, not 1,000 — the 1,000-5,000 figure is a "recommended for best results" range, not a hard floor. 850 genuine buyers is a legitimate, usable seed on its own.

**Better ways to grow it without diluting intent**

1. **Extend the purchase window** (180 or 365 days instead of 90) — same behavioral bar, just more of it.
2. **Add adjacent high-intent pixel/CAPI events**: add-to-cart, initiate-checkout, high-engagement product-page viewers. Closer in intent to "buyer" than "subscriber."
3. **Use a value-based Lookalike** seeded on purchase value (via CAPI), not a plain 1% lookalike — this skews toward your best customers, which matters more for a premium product than raw volume.
4. **If you use the email list at all, filter to clickers, not openers.** A click requires a real action and survives MPP; an open mostly doesn't.
5. **Keep it as a separate test**, not a merged seed: run one Lookalike off the 850 purchasers and a second off an "engaged" custom audience (site visitors, email clickers, IG/FB engagers). Compare performance instead of blending signal strengths you can't separate afterward.

**Bottom line:** don't pad the seed with weak signals just to hit a round number. Ship the 850-purchaser lookalike now, and grow it with same-intent behavioral data (longer window, ATC/checkout, value-based) — not newsletter reach or a metric (email opens) that's been broken since 2021.
