# Interview Prep: Growth Marketer, Fintech ($150k, Final Round + Live Account Audit)

## Quick facts

- Today: Sat Sep 12. Interview: Fri Sep 18. 6 days of runway.
- Format includes a live account audit — ask the recruiter now which tool/account (paid ads, GA4, product funnel) so you're not guessing on the day.

## What "live account audit" usually tests

They're not grading whether you find the "right" answer. They're grading whether you think out loud in a structured way under unfamiliar data — the same way you'd behave on day one with their real dashboards.

## Day-by-day prep plan

**Sat–Sun — Research**

- Company: product, ICP, pricing, funding stage, competitors, recent news.
- Regulatory footprint: bank charter or partner-bank model, state licensing, EU/US scope.
- Public growth signals: channels used, rough CAC/LTV proxies (SimilarWeb, App Annie, SEC filings if public).

**Mon — Build your audit toolkit**

- Pick 1–2 real accounts you can open live (your own ads account, a GA4 property, any funnel you own).
- Memorize the audit framework below until it's reflex, not notes.

**Tue — Narrative & questions**

- Prepare 2–3 STAR stories: a channel you scaled, a funnel you fixed, one failure with a lesson learned.
- Prepare fintech-specific proof points: compliance-safe creative, trust-building tactics, LTV:CAC in a regulated funnel.
- Draft 5–8 sharp questions for them.

**Wed — Mock live audit**

- Have someone hand you a random dashboard, or self-record, and narrate a 15–20 min audit out loud.
- Practice: structure first, findings second, recommendation third.

**Thu — Light review + logistics**

- One pass over notes, no cramming.
- Confirm screen-share setup and which account they'll show.
- Sleep well.

**Fri — Interview day**

- Log in 10 min early, test screen-share.
- Bring a 1-page cheat sheet (below).

## Live-audit framework — memorize this

1. **Clarify** — ask what KPI/goal matters before touching data.
2. **Orient** — funnel stage, channel mix, timeframe, what "normal" looks like here.
3. **Diagnose** — scan for CAC trend, conversion drop-offs, wasted spend, audience overlap, creative fatigue, compliance red flags in copy.
4. **Prioritize** — rank findings by impact × effort, not by discovery order.
5. **Recommend** — 2–3 concrete actions, each with expected impact and how you'd measure it.
6. **Narrate throughout** — the thinking is what's graded, not the final number.

## Fintech-specific angles to weave in unprompted

- **Disclosure compliance**: APR/fee language in ad copy, "not FDIC insured" for investment products, state-by-state licensing limits on geo-targeting.
- **Trust signals**: security badges, testimonials, press mentions — fintech conversion is trust-gated harder than most verticals.
- **KYC/identity verification drop-off**: a fintech-unique funnel leak; flag it even if not asked.
- **LTV horizon**: lending/banking LTV is long and cohort-based — don't over-index on 30-day ROAS.
- **Platform ad policy**: Google/Meta financial-services certification and targeting restrictions — showing awareness here signals real fintech experience.

## Scorecard — what they'll likely grade you on

| Category                         | Weight | Strong signal                                                                  | Red flag                                                    |
| -------------------------------- | ------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ----------------------------------------------------------- |
| Structured thinking (live audit) | 25%    | States a framework up front, hypothesis-driven, prioritized findings           | Random clicking, jumps to conclusions                       |
| Fintech/compliance fluency       | 15%    | Flags regulatory/trust issues unprompted                                       | Generic e-commerce playbook, no regulatory awareness        |
| Metrics fluency                  | 15%    | Fluent in CAC, LTV, payback period, cohorts; reads the dashboard correctly     | Misreads basic metrics, confuses correlation with causation |
| Channel/tactical expertise       | 15%    | Specific, current tactics per channel                                          | Buzzwords, no specifics                                     |
| Communication under pressure     | 10%    | Narrates clearly, stays calm on unfamiliar data, admits uncertainty gracefully | Goes silent, bluffs, over-hedges                            |
| Business judgment/prioritization | 10%    | Ties recommendations to business impact and effort                             | Laundry list, no ranking                                    |
| Culture/collaboration signal     | 5%     | Asks good clarifying questions, coachable tone                                 | Defensive, dismissive of interviewer's data                 |
| Questions asked to them          | 5%     | Sharp, specific to their growth stage                                          | Generic or none                                             |

## Print-and-carry cheat sheet

- Framework: **Clarify → Orient → Diagnose → Prioritize → Recommend → Narrate.**
- 3 fintech risk flags to always check: disclosure language, KYC drop-off, geo/licensing limits.
- 3 metrics to always mention: CAC payback period, LTV:CAC by cohort, post-signup activation rate.
- 2–3 STAR stories ready, one must include a failure and the lesson.
- 5–8 questions ready for them.
