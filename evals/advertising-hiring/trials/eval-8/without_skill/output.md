## 2. Turnover rate for backfill modeling

- **Performance marketing / paid media specifically: ~20–30% annual voluntary turnover** — higher than general corporate/professional roles (~15-18%) due to high external demand, agency-poaching, and burnout from always-on channel management.
- **Agency-side** paid media talent churns faster (25-35%+); **in-house** senior roles run lower (15-22%).
- For backfill-risk modeling on a single senior in-house hire, **25% annual attrition risk** is a reasonable planning assumption, with **60-90 days time-to-fill** given how tight specialized paid-media talent is.
