# Quillwater — Performance Marketer 30-60-90, with the access-timeline conflict resolved

## ⚠️ Flag this to the COO first

Two of the COO's asks collide with IT's access schedule. Worth surfacing before locking the plan:

| Ask                              | Requires                         | Available on         |
| -------------------------------- | -------------------------------- | -------------------- |
| CAC target commitment, week 2    | Live/historical performance data | Day 60 (analytics)   |
| Scale best campaign 50%, month 1 | Ad account access                | Day 30 (ad accounts) |

- Day 30 is when ad access **starts** — not enough runway left in "month 1" to scale and show a result.
- A CAC commitment in week 2 with zero data access is a guess dressed as a target. Holding someone accountable to a number they can't yet see or influence isn't real accountability.
- Below is a version of both asks that's achievable and still ambitious, plus what I'd ask the COO/IT to unlock instead.

## 🎯 Reframed asks

- **Week 2:** present a **CAC hypothesis/range** (not a commitment), built from historical exports, finance's LTV assumptions, and category benchmarks. Convert to a **committed target on Day 75** — two weeks into real analytics access, enough to see a live trend.
- **Month 1:** the "win" is a **fully-built, pre-approved scaling plan** ready to fire the second ad access unlocks — not a completed scale-and-prove cycle. Execute Day 30-31, first credible read by Day 45.
- **Also flag:** scaling a campaign 50% in one shot risks resetting the platform's learning phase (Meta/Google) and spiking CPA right when it's being watched hardest. Recommend staging it — e.g. two ~25% bumps a few days apart — so 50% is reached without torching efficiency.

## Days 1-30 — no ad or analytics access yet

**Goal:** arrive Day 30 with a scaling plan pre-approved and zero ramp time wasted.

- Week 1: stakeholder rounds (COO, product, finance, current/outgoing marketer), review brand/ICP/funnel, request read-only exports of historical campaign + CAC/LTV data from whoever currently holds access.
- Week 1-2: audit tracking setup (pixel/MMP — AppsFlyer, Adjust, etc.) secondhand via docs and the data team, not live login.
- Week 2: deliver the CAC **hypothesis range** to the COO (not a commitment) with sources shown.
- Week 3: identify the best-performing campaign from historical exports, draft the 50% scale plan (budget ramp schedule, creative/audience checks), get COO sign-off.
- Week 4: competitive + creative audit as a standalone quick win — doesn't need any access, gives the COO something tangible before Day 30.
- Day ~25-30: onboarding/security probation should close; confirm ad account credentials are queued to land exactly Day 30.

✅ Day 30 checkpoint: scaling plan approved and ready to execute same day access lands.

## Days 31-60 — ad account access, still no analytics

**Goal:** execute the scale, read platform-native numbers with a grain of salt.

- Day 30-31: launch the staged 50% budget ramp on the top campaign.
- Days 31-45: monitor platform-reported CPA/ROAS only — flag to COO that platform numbers tend to over-credit their own conversions vs. true attribution, so this isn't the final scorecard.
- ⏳ Ask the data/analytics team for a **manual weekly export** during this window, so the scale decision isn't flying blind for a full 30 days.
- Day 45: first informal readout to COO on the scale — directional, caveated as platform-only data.
- Days 45-60: extend budget testing to 1-2 secondary campaigns using the same staged approach; start drafting Q-next channel plan.

## Days 61-90 — full access, closed loop

**Goal:** validate the scale with real data, commit to and own the CAC number.

- Day 60: analytics access lands — reconcile platform-reported vs. true CAC/LTV on the scaled campaign.
- Day 61-70: correct course if platform data misled the Day 45 read; this is the first fully-informed decision point.
- **Day 75: commit to the real CAC target**, owned going forward, with two weeks of live data behind it.
- Day 75-90: propose next scaling candidate, present a 90-day retro (what the access gap cost, if anything) and the next quarter's roadmap.

## Questions worth putting to COO/IT before Day 1

- Can ad account access be **shadow/co-managed** earlier (new hire directs, a current team member's login executes) instead of waiting the full 30 days?
- Can **read-only** analytics access be scoped and granted sooner than full access, even if edit rights wait until Day 60?
- If neither is negotiable: get explicit COO sign-off now that the "big win" lands ~Day 45 and the CAC commitment lands Day 75, so expectations don't quietly reset to the original asks later.
