**Not signing off as-is — the retargeting shift is the problem, not the budget pull.**

## What's fine
- Pulling $60K forward from December: neutral on its own, *if* December has no comparable demand spike. For DTC consumer electronics this is a real if — check last year's Dec revenue/ROAS by week before moving the money. If Dec has its own holiday/gifting surge, you may be robbing a higher-intent period to fund a lower one. Verify, don't assume.
- Ramping spend into a known high-intent window: directionally correct.

## What's not fine

**⚠️ Flat 40% raise "every campaign," all at once**
- Triggers Meta/Google learning-phase resets on affected ad sets right before the event you need them stable for.
- November is also when every other retailer ramps for BFCM — CPMs rise regardless of your own budget move, so 40% more spend ≠ 40% more reach.
- Fix: stagger the increase over 5-7 days pre-event, not a single jump the week before.

**🔴 Shifting prospecting into retargeting during the event — this is backwards**
- Retargeting can only harvest the audience prospecting already built. Cutting prospecting *during* the event shrinks the very pool you're trying to convert, right when you need it largest.
- Retargeting spend mechanically produces higher ROAS than prospecting almost every time — because it's converting people already close to purchase (including some who'd have bought anyway via direct/branded search/email). Shifting budget toward it will make blended ROAS look better **whether or not it's actually incremental revenue.**
- That's almost certainly what happened last year. "Blended ROAS looked great" is the expected artifact of this exact move — it's not evidence the strategy worked, it's evidence the metric is easy to game.
- Net effect: you likely bought a good-looking dashboard number by suppressing new-customer acquisition during your best acquisition window of the quarter. That cost shows up later — smaller Q1 retention/LTV base, weaker December remarketing pool.

## What I'd approve instead
1. **Build the retargeting pool before the event, not during it.** Increase prospecting spend in the 2-3 weeks *before* the sale so the retargeting pool is maximally full going in.
2. **During the event, tilt — don't gut.** Shift retargeting weight modestly (e.g. +10-15pp of mix), keep prospecting flowing so you're still acquiring new customers at peak intent, not just harvesting.
3. **Gate the "win" on new-customer volume and CAC, not blended ROAS alone.** Blended ROAS is easy to inflate; net new customers and true incremental revenue are what Q4 targets and Dec/Q1 LTV actually depend on.
4. **Run a holdout or geo lift test during the event window** if you don't already have one, specifically on the retargeting shift — this is the only way to know if last year's ROAS bump was real lift or cannibalized organic/direct/email conversions getting relabeled as paid.
5. **Confirm December's historical demand curve before pulling the $60K** — if Dec is strong for you, pull from the weakest-performing Nov lines instead, not from Dec.

**Bottom line:** sign off on the budget pull pending the Dec check, reject the "shift most prospecting into retargeting" mechanic as written, approve a milder tilt with prospecting protected, and put an incrementality test in place before you trust the ROAS number this time.
