# Verdict: don't finalize this plan — three of its five moves work against you

Your instinct to move money toward the higher-ROAS lines is the standard trap. Blended platform ROAS isn't comparable across channels, and two of your "best performers" have structural ceilings the raw multiplier hides. Here's the math and the fix.

## Why the plan backfires

**1. Brand search's 12x is not a scaling signal — it's an attribution artifact.**
Brand search captures people already typing your name into Google — much of that demand converts via organic/direct for free if the ad isn't there. Google's self-reported ROAS on brand terms is structurally inflated because it's crediting your own existing customers finding you. Doubling spend here doesn't create demand, it just pays for clicks you may already own — **and you have no impression-share data for brand** (unlike non-brand, where you _do_ have it). If brand IS is already >90%, most of the extra $12K literally cannot spend — there's no auction left to win.

**2. Retargeting can't absorb a 75% budget jump — the pool is fixed size.**
4.9x ROAS is real, but retargeting audience size is a function of _upper-funnel traffic_, which prospecting generates. Taking $12K from prospecting and dumping it into retargeting doesn't grow the retargeting pool — it just increases frequency against the same people, which caps out fast (frequency fatigue, rising CPMs) and starts converting people who were going to buy anyway. Its 4.9x is also attribution-inflated for the same reason as brand search.

**3. Prospecting isn't underperforming — it's under-saturated.**
You've reached 410K of a 2.4M addressable audience in 30 days (~17%). That's nowhere near frequency ceiling. A 2.0x ROAS against 52% contribution margin isn't "losing to Meta" — it's the _engine that feeds_ retargeting and brand search next month. Gut it 40% and you won't see the damage this month; you'll see it in retargeting pool size and brand search volume in 60–90 days.

**4. You're ignoring the one channel that's explicitly telling you to spend more.**
Non-brand search losing 44% impression share to budget is the single cleanest, lowest-risk signal in this whole account — it means proven, converting auctions you're simply not showing up for. Your plan cuts everything _except_ this line. That's backwards.

**5. Killing TikTok at week 5 of an 8-week, day-60-decision test breaks your own methodology.**
Many platforms are still exiting the learning phase at week 5. Ending early on an incomplete read means you'll never know if the day-60 verdict would've flipped — you'll have spent $40K on a test and thrown away the data point at the finish line.

**6. "4x ROAS overall" isn't grounded in your economics.**
Breakeven ROAS at 52% margin is **1.92x** (1 ÷ 0.52). A blanket 4x target is an arbitrary industry benchmark, not derived from your contribution margin — it implicitly demands ~27% margin-on-revenue after media from every channel uniformly, which ignores that prospecting's job is new-customer acquisition (LTV), not same-day payback.

## Before you lock this in, pull three numbers

1. **Brand search impression share (not just ROAS).** If IS is already ≥90%, keep the $10K (or trim further — the rest is likely unspendable anyway). If there's real headroom, put the $2K back into brand instead of the trim, and take it from prospecting instead (→ $22K).
2. **Non-brand search's actual ROAS.** I sized the +$6K assuming its marginal ROAS holds near its current average — reasonable for a budget-capped (not audience-saturated) channel, but confirm before scaling further next month.
3. **TikTok's actual ROAS trend, not just "hasn't beaten 2.0x."** A flat-to-declining trend at week 5 and a rising one heading toward 2.0x are different signals even if today's snapshot looks the same. Use the day-60 date as planned either way.

## What to watch after the shift

- **Non-brand search CPCs/ROAS as spend scales** — confirm marginal efficiency holds past the current bid stack before adding more next round.
- **Retargeting frequency/CPMs** — first sign of pool saturation from the modest $2K add.
- **Prospecting reach %** — should keep climbing toward saturation even at $24K; if it stalls, that's your next cut signal, not ROAS alone.
- **Day 60 TikTok readout** — pre-commit the kill/scale threshold now so it's not relitigated in the moment.
