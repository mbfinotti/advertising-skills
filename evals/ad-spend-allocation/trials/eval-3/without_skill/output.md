## Why this split, not an even split of the freed $32.4K

- **Search gets the largest increase** because it's the only line with a *quantified, mechanical* growth lever: 38% of search impression share is currently lost to budget, not to targeting or market size. That's near-guaranteed incremental volume, not a bet.
- **Review-site listings get a smaller bump.** It's part of the channel pair (with search) that sourced 63% of pipeline on 45% of spend — strong combined efficiency — but we don't have listing-level attribution isolated from search, and premium directory placements have a hard inventory ceiling. Scaling this one aggressively risks paying for slots that don't exist.
- **Webinar promotion gets the largest % increase off a small base.** It co-drove last month's 6 closed-won deals and was the most budget-starved of the working channels — cheap to test scaling registration spend further.
- **LinkedIn kept at $21.6K, not zero.** CEO asked for a 60% cut, not elimination — honored as stated.

## ⚠️ Caveat before you present this upward

Last month's "zero from LinkedIn" reflects deals that **closed** last month — given the 105-day sales cycle, those were **sourced ~3.5 months ago**, under whatever targeting/creative was running back then, not under current LinkedIn spend. It's a lagging indicator being used to judge current performance. Two consequences:

- This doesn't invalidate the cut — LinkedIn's reach is also thin (12,400 of 38,000, 33% in 30 days) and search/review already have a much tighter, causal case. The directional call is sound.
- But it does mean the $21.6K remaining on LinkedIn shouldn't be re-judged again next quarter using the same lagging metric. **Track pipeline-sourced (not closed-won) from LinkedIn going forward**, and re-evaluate the $21.6K based on that leading signal once this quarter's cohort has had time to mature — not at 30 days.

## 🎯 One thing to watch on the search increase

A +53% search budget increase to close the impression-share gap may pull in lower-intent queries at the margin. Monitor CPL and pipeline-sourced quality on the incremental spend, not just impression share recovered — diminishing returns are likely before you hit 0% lost share.
