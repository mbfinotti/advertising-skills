# Casaverde — Google Search Ads Brief (RSA)

## ⚠️ Two things in the plan will fight each other

**1. Search captures intent; it doesn't create it.**
Casaverde is a new category with ~zero search volume for "smart compost concierge" or anything close to it. RSAs only fire on queries people actually type. No query stream → no impressions, regardless of how good the copy is. Fix: target the *adjacent, already-searched* problem space (compost pickup, food waste collection, "I don't want to compost myself") instead of the category name. Consider briefing a parallel Demand Gen/YouTube push to build category awareness — search then captures the demand that awareness creates. Scoped this brief to Search only, per your ask, but flagging it since pure-Search-only will under-deliver on volume for a term nobody searches yet.

**2. Pinning all 6 headlines structurally blocks "Excellent" Ad Strength.**
Google's Ad Strength score rewards headline/description diversity and combination testing. Pinning every headline turns the RSA into a static ad and removes the variation the algorithm needs — Google's own guidance is to pin only when a claim is legally/compliance-required, never as default. In practice, fully-pinned RSAs cap out at "Average," rarely "Good," essentially never "Excellent." **Recommendation: write 12–15 headlines, pin only 1 (brand name, for consistency), leave the rest unpinned.** That's the only version of this brief that can hit Excellent while still guaranteeing your message anchor.

## Campaign structure (3 ad groups by intent, not by product feature)

| Ad group | Keyword theme | Example queries | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Problem-aware** | pain point, no brand awareness needed | "compost smells bad," "how to compost without a yard," "easiest way to compost at home" | Highest volume; people already frustrated |
| **Service-adjacent** | substitute services that exist today | "compost pickup service," "food waste collection service near me," "curbside composting" | Closest existing demand to your actual offer |
| **High-intent local** | ready to buy, geo-modified | "compost service [city]," "food scrap pickup [city]" | Lowest volume, highest conversion — protect budget here |

Start Broad Match + Smart Bidding (Maximize Conversions, no tCPA yet — too little data) with audience signals (in-market: home & garden, life events: recently moved/new homeowner) layered on. Add Phrase/Exact only once you have impression data — there isn't a stable exact-match keyword list for this yet.

## 🎯 3 hooks for the search ads

**1. Effort-elimination hook** — *"You don't compost. We compost so you don't have to."*
Lands because the barrier to composting is never belief, it's labor. Removes the objection in one line.

**2. Guilt-to-relief hook** — *"That banana peel could've been soil. Now it is."*
Reframes a daily small waste moment into a solved problem — works as a headline or as the emotional core of the description copy.

**3. Proof-of-service hook** — *"The compost bin that tells you it's working."*
Leans on "smart" being the actual differentiator against every other compost method (including doing nothing) — gives skeptics a concrete reason to trust a service category they've never heard of.

## Iteration loop to reach Excellent

1. Publish with 1 pinned + 14 unpinned headlines, 4 descriptions.
2. Check Ad Strength per ad group, not account-wide — problem-aware and service-adjacent groups will score differently.
3. If capped at "Good": add 2–3 more headline variants using each ad group's top keyword theme verbatim (Google flags missing keyword-relevance first).
4. Never let pinned headlines exceed 2 of 15 — re-check every time you're tempted to lock more copy in.
5. Re-evaluate weekly for the first month; RSA scoring shifts as it collects impression data, not just from edits.
