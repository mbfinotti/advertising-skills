Before the brief itself — four things in your plan will cause real problems, flagging per your convention:

🔴 **Skipping disclosure hashtags is a compliance gap, not just a style choice.** Meta's "Sponsored" label satisfies Meta's ad-transparency rule. It does **not** satisfy the FTC's material-connection disclosure requirement, which sits on the _creator's own content_ independent of how the platform labels the ad. Have legal counsel confirm before you send anything — I've built the disclosure requirement into the brief below rather than omitting it.

⚠️ **A word-for-word identical script across 4 creators kills the reason you're paying for UGC.** Authenticity is the entire value proposition, and near-duplicate creative across accounts also tends to get flagged/suppressed by Meta's ad system as repetitive. I've replaced the script with a shared messaging guide (required beats + must-say claims) that each creator delivers in their own words.

⚠️ **"Unlimited perpetual rights" isn't actually standard — it's a significant ask.** Most creators either price it much higher or decline outright, and disputes over open-ended usage are one of the most common sources of creator/brand friction. I've flagged a defined term (recommend 12 months, renewable) for legal to confirm rather than baking in "perpetual."

ℹ️ **Three people sending notes to creators ad hoc will produce conflicting feedback and stall revisions.** I've routed brand/product/legal input through one point of contact who consolidates it into a single round.

5. CTA with promo code

**Must-say:** [product-lead-approved claims list — attach]
