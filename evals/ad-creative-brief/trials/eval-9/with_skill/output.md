## Before this runs

1. **Compliance gate, not a nice-to-have:** "vet-formulated" is a factual, checkable claim about your product's history. If it isn't literally true — a licensed vet was actually involved in formulation — it can't run as written; that's a substantiation problem the brief can't paper over, unlike a soft claim like "science-backed." Get the credential before this goes to a designer or creator.
2. **Evidence mining is deferred, not deleted.** Skipping the day-long scored review mining is a reasonable call for a first campaign under time pressure — but the cheapest evidence rung (pulling top 1–2 star objections, ~1 hour) is worth doing before the second concept, not the tenth: it's what tells you whether "vet-formulated science" is actually the top objection this audience has, or whether the founder's instinct is aimed at the wrong wall.
3. **Competitor board, once you send the screenshots:** happy to board them by hook type and angle for hook-direction inspiration (Section 5, not built here) — just don't cite "competitors are doing this" as evidence in Section 3; recency isn't a validation signal, only longevity is.
