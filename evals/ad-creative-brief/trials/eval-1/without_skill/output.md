# Creative Brief — Meta Campaign, Q2

**Client:** Loomora | **Category:** Sleep supplement (DTC) | **Platform:** Meta (Facebook + Instagram) | **Objective:** Awareness + Sales | **Prepared for:** Design team

## 2. Flag before you start (read this first)

Two structural calls I made so I could deliver the brief now instead of stalling on questions — override either if wrong:

1. **"All four points in" applies to the campaign, not every single ad.** Cramming 4 claims into one static image reliably tanks stopping power and CTR — Meta creative that leads with one clear hook consistently outperforms multi-claim ads. So: **every ad leads with ONE pillar as the hook**, and carries the other three as a lightweight proof stack (icon row / lower-third) so nothing is dropped. Across the campaign's ad set, all four pillars get equal hero rotation. If leadership specifically wants all four in the *same frame* of every creative, say so and I'll redesign the system.
2. **"Cheaper than prescription alternatives" needs careful phrasing.** Meta's health/wellness ad policy and FTC rules restrict supplements from implying they're a substitute for, or equivalent to, prescription medication (this reads as an efficacy/treatment claim, which supplements legally can't make). Naming specific drugs (e.g. Ambien, Lunesta) also raises rejection risk. Use **price framing, not efficacy framing** — see Section 7.

Unknowns I assumed defaults for — flag if wrong: no specific promo/discount code, no fixed budget split given, no landing page specified (assumed existing loomora.com PDP/quiz flow), no existing brand guideline doc referenced (assumed none exists yet).

## 3. Objective & KPIs

- **Awareness:** thumb-stop rate, 3-sec video views, CPM, reach
- **Sales:** ROAS, CPA, add-to-cart rate, purchase conversion
- **Structure recommendation:** run as a single Advantage+ Sales campaign with broad targeting (let Meta's algorithm find the sub-segments within 25–60) rather than splitting into separate awareness and conversion campaigns — Meta's delivery system now handles full-funnel better than manual funnel-splitting for a catalog this size. Optimize for purchase; awareness is a byproduct of a broad, high-frequency buy.

---

## 4. Audience

**Stated target:** all adults 25–60, wellness-minded — genuinely broad. Don't over-segment creative by age/gender; instead let creative *variants* naturally appeal to different sub-audiences (see concepts below), and let Advantage+ targeting do the narrowing.

Loose reference personas for the designer's mental model (not separate campaigns):
- **The Skeptical Optimizer (30s–40s):** already tried melatonin, didn't like the grogginess/dependency feeling. Responds to "melatonin-free" + lab testing.
- **The Price-Conscious Swapper (40s–60s):** on or considering a prescription sleep aid, price-sensitive, wants "as effective, way cheaper."
- **The Social Proof Follower (25–60, broad):** decides based on "40,000 people can't be wrong."

---

## 5. Message Architecture

| Pillar | Core claim | Emotional job |
|---|---|---|
| Melatonin-free | No dependency, no grogginess, no messing with your hormones | Reassurance / differentiation |
| Third-party lab tested | Independently verified, not just brand claims | Trust |
| Cheaper than Rx alternatives | Real savings, no doctor/insurance hassle | Value / permission to switch |
| 40,000+ customers | You're not the guinea pig | Social proof |

**Rule for every ad:** 1 hero pillar in the hook (headline + first 2 sec of video / dominant text on static) + the other 3 as a compact proof row (small icons/badges: 🧪 3rd-party tested · 💰 vs. Rx pricing · ⭐ 40,000+ customers) so the full claim set is always present, just hierarchized.

---

## 6. Campaign / Creative Set Structure

Build **4 hero creative directions**, one per pillar as the lead hook, each produced in 3 formats. This gives Meta's algorithm enough variety to find winners and covers all four mandated claims across the set.

| Format | Spec | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Feed static/carousel | 1:1 (1080×1080) or 4:5 (1080×1350) | Primary — highest volume |
| Stories/Reels | 9:16 (1080×1920), safe zone: keep text/logo 250px from top & bottom | Video-first, hook must land in first 1.5 sec |
| Video feed | 1:1 or 4:5, 15–30 sec | UGC/testimonial style |

Text overlay on static images: keep under ~20% of frame for delivery efficiency (Meta no longer hard-blocks on this, but overlay-heavy images still get throttled in auction).

## 7. Creative Concepts (for designer)

### Concept A — "No Melatonin, No Problem" (hero: melatonin-free)
Clean product shot, bold headline overlay. Visual metaphor: crossed-out melatonin molecule icon or a "groggy morning" vs "clear morning" split image. Tone: confident, slightly contrarian ("Everyone else uses melatonin. We didn't want to.").

### Concept B — "Lab-Verified, Not Just Brand-Promised" (hero: 3rd-party tested)
Show the actual Certificate of Analysis / lab badge as a design element — QR code linking to results is a strong trust device. Visual: product + lab report mockup, magnifying glass or checkmark motif. Tone: credible, slightly clinical, no hype.

### Concept C — "Why Pay for a Prescription?" (hero: price)
**Do not name specific Rx drugs or claim medical equivalence.** Frame as a straightforward cost comparison: "$X/month vs. average $Y/month for a prescription sleep aid + doctor visit + copay" — cite a generic category ("prescription sleep medication"), not a brand. Visual: simple price comparison graphic, calculator/receipt motif. This is the ad most likely to get flagged by Meta review — keep copy factual and price-only, zero efficacy language.

### Concept D — "40,000+ People Sleep Better With This" (hero: social proof)
UGC-style or review-wall visual — real customer quotes, star ratings, a counter-style "40,000+" as the dominant number. Best as video (real customer testimonial, 15–20 sec) or a static review-collage carousel. Tone: warm, real, unpolished (UGC outperforms polished product shots for trust-building).

Each concept's secondary proof row carries the other three claims as small badges/icons — same badge system reused across all four for visual consistency.

## 9. Visual & Tone Direction

- **Palette/type:** follow existing Loomora brand kit if one exists — flag to me if you want help establishing one, since none was referenced.
- **Mood:** calm, credible, a little warm — not clinical-pharma, not woo-wellness. Think "trusted friend with a lab coat," not "influencer supplement ad."
- **Avoid:** before/after body imagery, "cure," "treat," or "fix your insomnia" language (regulatory risk), stock-photo sleep clichés (person peacefully asleep in white linen — overused across the category).

## 11. Compliance Guardrails (non-optional)

- No disease/condition treatment claims ("cures insomnia," "fixes sleep disorders") — supplements can only support, not treat.
- No named prescription drugs in copy or imagery.
- Price comparisons must stay price-only — never imply equal or superior efficacy to medication.
- Lab-testing claim must be backed by an actual, current Certificate of Analysis you can link/show if Meta requests substantiation during ad review.
- "40,000+ customers" must be a real, current, defensible number at time of launch.

---

## 12. Success Metrics (Q2)

- Primary: ROAS and CPA against current baseline
- Secondary: reach/frequency, video 3-sec view rate (awareness proxy), CTR by pillar (tells you which claim resonates most — useful for Q3 budget reallocation)
