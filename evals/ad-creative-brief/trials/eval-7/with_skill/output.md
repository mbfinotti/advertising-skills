## Flags before this goes live

Three parts of what you specified deviate from the skill's attributed benchmarks. Flagging them so they're a knowing call, not a silent gap:

⚠️ **Cross-platform benchmarking.** The skill is explicit: _"hook rate is 3-second plays ÷ impressions on Meta but TikTok counts at 2 seconds — never benchmark across platforms."_ Your TikTok agency's 32% is calibrated to a 2-second window; holding Meta (3-second window, structurally harder to clear) to the same number stacks the deck against every Meta file. If you want one shared bar for ops simplicity, that's a legitimate call — but expect Meta files to under-clear it more often for reasons that have nothing to do with creative quality. Consider either a slightly lower Meta-specific bar or reading Meta hook rate as a trend vs. account history rather than an absolute pass/fail at 32%.

⚠️ **Minimum evidence.** Published starting point (SparkUGC, cited in the skill): **≥2,000 impressions** before reading a hook rate. 600–900 by Wednesday is well under that — at that volume a handful of scroll-happy viewers can swing the percentage several points either way. Not a reason to delay the read (Wednesday is a reasonable cadence for a 6-file test), but treat a Wednesday-morning number in the 25–35% band as noisy, not decisive — don't kill on a 1-2pt miss.

⚠️ **Mid-week audience swap.** This is the failure mode the skill names directly: _"Changing targeting mid-test — the test variable is no longer the only variable... Fix: freeze targeting for the test window; targeting changes are their own test."_ The table above resolves it by treating the swap as a fresh test cell rather than a continuation of the same read — so you get the "fair second chance" you want without quietly corrupting the original hook-rate number. Just make sure whoever executes the swap actually renames the asset and doesn't report the blended number.

Everything else in the block reflects what you specified as given.
