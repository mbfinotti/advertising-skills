## Notes

- Every headline leads with **Peakform** per your brand-building priority.
- Because the audience doesn't yet link back pain to sitting posture, each hook opens by naming a familiar culprit (mattress, age, workouts) and correcting it — that's the "aha" that makes the category legible before you ever explain the product mechanics.
- Emojis are capped at 1 per asset (🎯 or 💪) so they read as accents, not clutter — LinkedIn's B2B-adjacent audience tolerates fewer than Instagram's.
- The 60 Trustpilot reviews won't fit inside these character limits without crowding out the education hook — better used as: a 3rd carousel card ("4.6★ · 60 reviews"), a landing-page trust bar right under the headline, or a retargeting variant once the audience already knows what Peakform is.
