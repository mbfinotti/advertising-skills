## Delivery notes

- **Read direction**: conversational, matter-of-fact — she's describing her week, not endorsing a brand. No "testimonial voice."
- **Setting**: Harber warehouse floor + a short desk cutaway. Existing lighting is fine — native/unpolished outperforms polished on this placement.
- **Hold up to camera**: the reconciliation report (or laptop) at the outcome beat and in hook 1 — that's the checkable receipt, don't lose it to a cutaway.
- **Captions on throughout** — assume sound off.
- **Shoot format**: phone on a tripod, 30–45 minutes, during a call or site visit. No crew needed — this is what keeps it inside 3 weeks.
- **Do**: record the outcome line 3–4 times so you can pick the most natural take; let her finish sentences her own way; grab extra b-roll (walking the floor, using a scanner) for cutaways.
- **Don't**: feed her marketing words ("seamless," "game-changing"); over-rehearse — a slightly imperfect take reads more credible here than a polished one; cut away from her face during the outcome number.

## Compliance

- **Markets**: assumed US only — FTC Endorsement Guides apply (16 CFR 255). If this runs in the EU/France, add the language-specific label; say so and I'll rewrite the disclosure line.
- **Material connection**: none apparent — she's a genuine customer, not a paid actor or employee. Confirm whether Harber got anything (discount, free months) for the case study; if yes, add an on-screen line: `Harber Logistics received [X] for sharing this story`.
- **Every claim traceable**: the only documented fact is 14→3 hrs. Everything else in the script is proposed dialogue for Marisol to confirm, not a pre-existing quote — don't ship it as "what she said" until she's actually said it.
- **Typicality**: 14→3 hrs is a large, specific swing. If it's not typical across your customer base, add on-screen: `Individual results vary by warehouse size and SKU volume.` If it _is_ typical, you can drop this.
- **Sign-off**: get Marisol/Harber's final approval on the cut before it goes live — a signed case study covers the case study, not automatically the ad edit.

## Measurement plan

- **Variable under test**: hook (4 variants), body and CTA held fixed.
- **Baseline**: no existing LinkedIn video data for Ostrafell — treat this flight as the baseline-setting round rather than measuring against a published benchmark.
- **Diagnose in order**: hook rate (3s plays ÷ impressions) → hold rate (15s plays ÷ 3s plays) → CTR → demo-booked conversion. Fix whichever stage is weak; don't touch the others until it's fixed.
