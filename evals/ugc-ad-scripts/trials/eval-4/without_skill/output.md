### Shot list / production notes

**Founder's two segments (0:04–0:12 and 0:44–0:54):**
- Shoot both separately, as many takes as needed — they're short enough to nail without a "performance."
- Plain wall or the warehouse floor behind, natural light, phone on a tripod, external clip-mic (not the phone's built-in mic).
- Read from cue cards just off-lens, not memorized — it's fine, nobody will know.

**Marisol's segment (0:22–0:34):**
- Ask her to self-record on her phone, vertical, one take, answering: *"What changed after you started using Ostrafell — in your own words, with the before/after number."*
- Don't over-direct this. The slightly rougher, selfie-style footage reads as more credible than a lit interview, and it means zero scheduling dependency on your end.

**Screen recordings (0:12–0:22 and 0:34–0:44):**
- Can be captured any time, independent of both people's schedules — do this first so it's not a bottleneck.

**Captions:** burn in every line of dialogue. Auto-caption in CapCut or LinkedIn's native editor, then hand-correct.
