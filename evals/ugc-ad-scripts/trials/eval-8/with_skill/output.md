## Delivery notes

- **Beat timings**: as above; total 1:15. Cut on beat, not mid-sentence — a slow build anywhere in the first 8s burns the only guaranteed attention you have.
- **Tone**: dry, mildly exasperated-then-relieved. Not enthusiastic-spokesperson energy — this is a creator explaining a hack to a friend, not reading ad copy.
- **Hold up to camera**: phone screen during every "Desire" beat — don't cut away to B-roll for the feature beats, the screen recording *is* the proof.
- **What to avoid recording**: no delivery-truck stock footage, no "as seen on" badges, no invented numeric savings claim ("saves you 40%") unless Trundlepost can substantiate that exact figure — see compliance below.
- **Logo handling** (the point flagged above): small corner bug from ~0:03, never a full-frame card until the outro at 1:13–1:15. Do not open on the animated sting as originally briefed — that's the specific pattern this skill's evidence names as the top cause of low hook rate.
- **Do**: direct address to lens, natural hand gesture toward the phone screen, jump cuts between feature beats to keep pace up across six features in 40 seconds.
- **Don't**: don't let any single feature beat run past ~8s — six features in one 75s ad only works if none of them get to breathe.
