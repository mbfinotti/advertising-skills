One quick flag before the script: opening on a logo sting actually fights the stat you cited. The 8-second window is exactly why performance creative (TikTok/Reels/Stories) almost never opens on a static/animated logo — it's dead air with no scroll-stopping content, and it's the single most-skipped 3 seconds on the platform. Branding sticks better woven into the hook (product-in-hand, on-screen text, sonic logo under the visual) and repeated at 2-3 touchpoints than front-loaded before anything happens. I've written it your way as instructed — consider A/B testing a hook-first cut against it.

Also: you didn't send me Trundlepost's actual six features, so I've used the standard parcel-forwarding set (address, consolidation, inspection, storage, repacking, tracking/customs). Swap in the real six if they differ — the AIDA skeleton and timing hold either way.
