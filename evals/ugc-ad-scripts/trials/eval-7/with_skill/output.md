# Maisonry — Paid Social Package: Gifted-Creator Video (reuse) + Hired-Actor Video

Two compliance corrections before the scripts — both change what ships, not just how it's worded:

1. **"Paid-partnership toggle + 'merci Maisonry' in the caption" does not clear this.** The checklist is explicit: a platform's paid-partnership label "may not be adequate on its own," a caption/bio mention "is not sufficient placement," and a vague credit like "thanks to [brand]" "does not communicate the relationship — name it." All three gaps apply here at once. Fix below.
2. **The hired-actor video has no disclosure plan at all in the brief.** A paid actor presented as a "delighted customer" must be disclosed **in the ad** as not an actual consumer (FTC Endorsement Guides, 16 CFR 255). Without that, this isn't a weak script — it's the exact violation the checklist names.

Neither video came with customer-review evidence, so voice-of-customer lines below are written from the brief alone. **Status: `UNVALIDATED — voice-of-customer not sourced.`** Swap in real quotes from the organic video's comments or reviews before locking final phrasing.

## Video 1 — Gifted creator video, reused as paid ad (US + France)

This is one locked asset — no reshoot, no hook variants to test. The only script work left is the disclosure insert and the caption, and both must be **added**, not left as-is, because "reposting a creator's organic content as a paid ad does not remove the disclosure requirement."

**Concept**: existing organic video, unboxing/rave format, creator received the box free (gifting program). Two market cuts needed — same footage, different overlay language, because the disclosure must be written "in the language of every market it runs in."

### Disclosure overlay — two options

**Option A (zero new filming, text-only):**

| Market | On-screen text | Placement |
|---|---|---|
| US | `#ad · Maisonry sent me this box to try` | 0–3s card, repeated as a small persistent corner tag for the full runtime |
| France | `Collaboration commerciale avec Maisonry — box reçue gratuitement` | 0–3s card, **plus a persistent small "Collaboration commerciale" tag visible for the entire video** (French law requires the label visible throughout the promotion, not just up top) |

**Option B (recommended — one new 5-second pickup line, not a reshoot):** ask the creator to record one extra line to cut in before her existing footage. This satisfies "spoken or on-screen" more robustly than text alone and survives platform review better.

- US spoken line: *"Maisonry sent me this box to try — here's what happened."*
- FR spoken line: *"Maisonry m'a envoyé cette box gratuitement — je vous montre."*

Keep Option A's on-screen tag either way — spoken and on-screen should stack, not substitute for each other.

### Caption (replaces the "merci Maisonry" plan — that line moves to the end, after the required label)

| Market | Caption |
|---|---|
| US | `Ad — Maisonry sent me this box to try. My honest take below.` |
| France | `Publicité — collaboration commerciale avec @Maisonry. Box reçue gratuitement, avis sincère. Merci Maisonry 💛` |

### Platform setting
Keep the paid-partnership toggle **on** in both markets — it's necessary, just not sufficient on its own.

### CTA variants (caption/link only — the video itself isn't touched)
- CTA A: `Shop the box — link in bio`
- CTA B: `See what's inside — link in bio`

## Video 2 — Hired actor, "delighted customer" unboxing (US + France)

**Concept**: paid actor, staged unboxing, home-organization pain → Maisonry box → organized shelf. Duration 20–25s. Offer not specified in the brief — CTA below assumes a first-box discount; swap in the real offer before locking.

### Hook variants (5 — body stays fixed; each first body line extends that hook's premise)

| # | Archetype | Spoken line | On-screen text | Opening visual |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Skeptic's turn | "I've tried three organizing kits before this one — all clutter, more boxes." | another organizing box? really | Actor holds the sealed box, unconvinced |
| 2 | Problem callout | "My linen closet hasn't closed properly in a year." | the closet that never shuts | Actor tugs a stuck, overstuffed closet door |
| 3 | POV | "POV: the box everyone's been talking about finally shows up." | the box everyone's talking about | Actor spots the box on the doorstep |
| 4 | Silent review *(asset-gated — unboxing footage already exists)* | none | unboxing Maisonry — just watch | Hands cutting tape, lifting the lid, product reveal |
| 5 | Before/after contrast *(asset-gated — cheap to shoot here since it's a staged shoot, and home organization isn't a restricted category)* | "Same shelf, five minutes apart." | before → after, 5 min | Split cut: cluttered shelf → organized shelf |

Flag on hook 5: confirm "5 minutes" is a setup time Maisonry can actually support for what's shown on screen — before/after numbers "must be honest and substantiated," even when the performer is disclosed as an actor.

### Body (fixed, PAS)

| Time | Spoken (example phrasing — actor can be directed more tightly than a real creator since the read is openly disclosed as an ad) | On-screen text | Visual |
|---|---|---|---|
| 3–8s | "Every 'organization system' I own is just more stuff to organize." | more boxes ≠ more order | Gestures at a cluttered shelf/bin pile |
| 8–13s | "Everything's labeled and sized for the shelf I actually have." | pre-sized for real shelves | Lifts labeled bins out, slots one onto the shelf |
| 13–17s | "It even tells you where each piece goes, with the card inside." | a layout card, not a guess | Close-up on the layout card, places second bin |
| 17–20s | "Five minutes in, this shelf finally looks like a shelf." | 5 minutes, real difference | Wide shot of the finished shelf |

### CTA variants (locked)

- **A**: Spoken *"Your first box is [X]% off — link's right here."* / On-screen `first box [X]% off · link below`
- **B**: Spoken *"Grab your first Maisonry box with the link below."* / On-screen `get your first box · link below`

### Non-consumer disclosure — mandatory, both language cuts

This runs on **every version**, in-language, and combines both channels — the same "clear and hard to miss" bar as the material-connection disclosure:

| Market | Persistent on-screen label (full runtime) | End-card / spoken line before CTA |
|---|---|---|
| US | `Dramatization · paid actor, not an actual customer` | "Quick note — I'm a paid actor, not a real Maisonry customer." |
| France | `Mise en scène · acteur rémunéré, pas un client réel` (match the actor's gender: *acteur/actrice*, *un client réel/une cliente réelle*) | "Petite précision — je suis un acteur rémunéré, pas un vrai client Maisonry." |

### Delivery notes
- Read direction: warm, relieved, grounded — "delighted" should land as satisfaction, not gleeful salesperson energy, or it reads as an ad in the first second.
- Setting: real-looking closet/shelf, not a showroom; stage the "before" mess visibly since it's disclosed as dramatized anyway.
- Hold the labeled bin and the layout card to camera in the 8–17s window.
- Cut every 2–4 seconds; no logo card or static frame first — open on the actor or the mess.
- **Never caption or label this "testimonial," "review," or "real customer story"** anywhere — ad text, caption, or the landing page the ad links to. That framing is exactly what the non-consumer disclosure exists to prevent.
- Don't let the actor state a specific unverified result ("saved me 10 hours") — dramatized or not, a quantified claim still needs substantiation.

## Measurement plan (both videos)

Single variable under test: **hook** (Video 1: none — asset is locked, only the caption/CTA text varies; Video 2: 5 hook variants against the one fixed body). Hold offer and body constant.

Baseline against this account's own trailing median hook rate and hold rate for the same placement, not a published benchmark. Diagnose in order and fix only the failing stage:
- Low hook rate → rewrite the failing hook, not the offer.
- Healthy hook rate, low hold rate → check the hook→body transition line first.
- Healthy hold, low CTR → the CTA line is the problem.
- Healthy CTR, low conversion → that's the offer or landing page, not this script.
