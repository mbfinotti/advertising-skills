# Vantoria — UGC Ad Concepts (3 concepts × 4 hooks, 15–20s each)

**Compliance flags before you shoot these** (2 real risks, easy fixes):
1. **"Insomnia" is a diagnosed medical condition.** A dietary supplement legally can't claim to treat/cure/prevent it. Use the word only as a relatable audience *question* (as scripted below) — never follow it with "this fixes your insomnia." Keep all benefit lines in structure/function terms ("helps you fall asleep faster," "supports sleep quality").
2. **The 61→89 sleep score is a single customer's anecdote.** Fine to use as a hook, but pair it with "individual results may vary" / "not independently verified" — don't present it as a typical or guaranteed outcome.
3. **Creators getting free bottles = a material connection.** Every video needs a spoken or on-screen disclosure ("Partnered with Vantoria" / #VantoriaPartner), not just a caption hashtag buried at the end.

All scripts below already bake in a disclosure + disclaimer line at the end.

## Concept 1 — "The Sleep Score Reveal" (data hook)

**4 hooks (first 3 sec, to camera or on-screen text):**
1. "My sleep score went from 61 to 89 in three weeks. I didn't change anything else."
2. "I'm about to show you the screenshots that made my sleep app think I got a new phone."
3. "POV: your smartwatch finally stops judging your sleep."
4. "I tracked my sleep for 21 days straight after trying this — here's the screen recording."

**Full script (using Hook 1):**
- **0–3s** — Close-up, phone in hand showing sleep app score. VO: *"My sleep score went from 61 to 89 in three weeks."* On-screen: `61 → 89`
- **3–7s** — Screen recording: two dated screenshots side by side. VO: *"I didn't change my workouts, my diet, nothing. Just added one thing before bed."* Cut to Vantoria bottle on nightstand.
- **7–12s** — Creator takes the capsule. VO: *"Magnesium glycinate — the form actually made for sleep, not the cheap stuff from the pharmacy aisle."* On-screen: `Magnesium Glycinate`
- **12–16s** — Creator in bed, lights off, phone face-down. VO: *"No jitters, no weird taste. Just... actually falling asleep."*
- **16–20s** — On-screen text: *"'I fall asleep before my podcast episode ends now.'" — marketplace review, June.* VO: *"Not just me."* CTA: *"Vantoria — link in bio."* Small on-screen text: *"Partnered with Vantoria. Individual results may vary; sleep score based on one customer's report."*

## Concept 2 — "3am vs Morning" (dramatic before/after)

**4 hooks:**
1. "Do you struggle to fall asleep?" *(spoken straight to camera — see compliance note above for word choice)*
2. "If you've laid awake doing math about how many hours you'll get if you fall asleep RIGHT NOW — this one's for you."
3. "3am me vs 7am me. Same night. One bottle."
4. "I used to dread bedtime. Not anymore."

**Full script (using Hook 1):**
- **0–3s** — Dim room, phone-flashlight-on-face lighting, tired/no makeup, creator to camera. VO: *"Do you struggle to fall asleep?"* On-screen: `3:14 AM`
- **3–6s** — Quick cuts: staring at ceiling, tossing, checking phone. VO: *"Because I used to lie there for hours."*
- **6–10s** — Hard cut/snap transition: same creator, bright window light, glowing, smiling, hair done. On-screen: `7:02 AM`. VO: *"Now I actually wake up feeling like this."*
- **10–14s** — Vantoria bottle in hand, pour into palm. VO: *"One thing changed — magnesium glycinate before bed, from Vantoria."*
- **14–18s** — On-screen text: *"'No morning grogginess — that was my whole fear.'" — marketplace review, May.* VO: *"Same fear. Didn't happen."*
- **18–20s** — CTA + disclosure on-screen: *"Vantoria · Partnered with Vantoria. Not intended to diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any disease. Individual results may vary."*

## Concept 3 — "The Reviews Sold Me" (social proof)

**4 hooks:**
1. "I read every single review before buying this. Here's what convinced me."
2. "Y'all weren't lying about this stuff."
3. "The reviews for this sleep supplement live in my head rent free."
4. "The review that sold me wasn't from an influencer — it was a random person on their site."

**Full script (using Hook 1):**
- **0–3s** — Creator scrolling phone, screen-recording of a reviews page. VO: *"I read every single review before buying this."* On-screen: `Real customer reviews ↓`
- **3–8s** — Zoom on review text: *"'I fall asleep before my podcast episode ends now.'" — June.* VO: *"This one got me."* Cut to creator laughing/nodding to camera.
- **8–13s** — Second review on-screen: *"'No morning grogginess — that was my whole fear.'" — May.* VO: *"That's literally the only reason I hadn't tried a sleep supplement before."*
- **13–17s** — Creator holds Vantoria bottle, kitchen counter, natural light. VO: *"So I tried Vantoria — magnesium glycinate, one capsule before bed."*
- **17–20s** — Creator dimming bedroom lights, content smile. VO: *"Now I get it."* On-screen CTA + disclosure: *"Vantoria · Partnered with Vantoria. Individual results may vary."*
