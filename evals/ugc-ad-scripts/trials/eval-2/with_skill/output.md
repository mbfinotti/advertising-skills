# Fernwell — UGC Script Package
**Concept:** first-time plant owners who keep killing plants (problem-aware) × "the app tells you instead of you guessing" × 7-day free trial, $5.99/mo. Format: paid creator, phone-in-hand, talking to camera at home with her own plants. Platform: short-form vertical feed. Target: 20s. **Validation: grounded** — spoken lines source from App Store reviews (March, April 2026) and an ad-comment; the most specific/unverifiable claim (quote #1) is displayed as a citation, not spoken.

Volume note: one concept, 3 hooks, 2 CTAs (6 edit combos from one shoot) — scaled to a single Friday booking rather than the skill's default 2-3 concepts, since there's one creator and one day.

## Hook variants (locked word-for-word)

| # | Archetype | Spoken line | On-screen text | Opening visual |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Problem callout | **"I have killed every single plant I've ever owned."** | `I killed every plant I owned` | Creator standing next to a visibly droopy/sad plant, deadpan |
| 2 | Skeptic's turn | **"I really thought I just didn't have a green thumb."** | `no green thumb here` | Creator holding up phone, scrolling past old dead-plant photos |
| 3 | POV | **"POV: your monstera's dying and you have no idea why."** | `when it's dying and you don't know why` | Close-up on a struggling plant, camera pans up to creator's worried face |

**Persistent disclosure label:** on-screen text `Paid partnership with Fernwell` — visible for the first 5 seconds minimum, reappearing at the CTA. Pair with the platform's own paid-partnership tag (the tag alone isn't enough).

## Body (locked word-for-word — full script, no ad-libbing)

Beat 1 (transition) changes per hook to extend its specific premise. Beats 2-4 are identical across all three hooks.

**If Hook 1 (Problem callout):**
> Beat 1 (2-6s): **"I wasn't checking on them. I was guessing."** — on-screen: `guessing ≠ plant care` — visual: creator shrugs, half-smile

**If Hook 2 (Skeptic's turn):**
> Beat 1 (2-6s): **"Turns out it wasn't me — I just wasn't checking."** — on-screen: `it wasn't me` — visual: creator sets phone down, looks at camera

**If Hook 3 (POV):**
> Beat 1 (2-6s): **"You're not checking on it. You're guessing."** — on-screen: `you're guessing, not checking` — visual: creator turns to camera, second-person direct address

**Shared beats 2-4, identical after any of the three hooks:**

| Time | Spoken (locked) | On-screen text | Visual |
|---|---|---|---|
| 6-13s | **"Fernwell tells me when to water and how much light each plant needs. I stopped guessing."** | `tells you exactly when to water` | Creator holds phone up, screen recording of the app's plant-care screen visible |
| 13-16s | **"My plants are actually alive. My friends noticed."** | `"I cried when my monstera died — this app would've saved it." — App Store review, March` | Creator smiling next to a healthy plant; review quote overlays as on-screen text card |
| 16-20s | **CTA — see variants below** | see below | Creator points at phone/camera |

Estimated runtime: Script 1 ≈ 20.8s · Script 2 ≈ 22s · Script 3 ≈ 21.2s (52-55 spoken words ÷ 150 wpm). All land inside the 15-25s paid-social band even though slightly over the literal "20 seconds" ask — trim isn't worth losing a full sentence at this length.

## Delivery notes

- **Read direction:** rueful, self-deprecating, talking to a friend — not a presenter voice. This is a confession, not a pitch.
- **Setting:** her actual home, her actual plants — ideally one that's visibly struggling (for beat 1) and one that's visibly healthy (for beat 3). Real light, no ring-light gloss.
- **Prop:** phone with the Fernwell app open to a real plant-care screen, held up steady for 2+ seconds during the solution beat so the UI reads on screen.
- **Pacing:** hard cut every 2-4 seconds, first frame is the hook visual — no logo card, no slow build.
- **Do:** say "$5.99" and "7-day" exactly as written — these are regulated-adjacent figures, not paraphrase targets.
- **Do:** film all 3 hooks + both CTAs as pickup lines in one session against the same fixed body — this is one shoot producing 6 tested edits.
- **Do:** screen-record herself opening the app at some point Friday, even if unused in this cut — unlocks the silent-review archetype next round for free.
- **Don't:** say "my monstera died" as her own experience unless it's literally true for her — swap in whatever plant she's actually killed, or leave it as the on-screen citation only.
- **Don't:** drop the "Paid partnership with Fernwell" on-screen label — required visible for the first 5 seconds minimum.
- **Don't:** add "today only" or any deadline language to either CTA — the 7-day trial is a standing offer, not a countdown.

## Compliance

- **Market assumed:** US, English. If this also runs in the EU or France, add the French "collaboration commerciale" label (visible throughout, in French) and confirm DSA labeling — flag before it ships to those markets.
- **Material connection:** she's paid per video → disclosed via persistent on-screen label + platform paid-partnership tag (checklist requires both; toggle alone is explicitly insufficient).
- **Genuine use required:** she is presented as an actual user, not an actor — she needs to install Fernwell and run the free trial before filming, not just read lines about it.
- **Quote traceability:** quote #2 and #3 informed her own spoken lines (paraphrase, not verbatim attribution) — confirm with her beforehand that "I stopped guessing" / "my friends noticed" are true for her. Quote #1 is shown as a dated, sourced citation rather than spoken, so it doesn't require her personal confirmation.
- **No regulated-category gate:** plant care isn't health/finance/supplements, so no extra substantiation review beyond standard FTC endorsement disclosure.
- **Script-pass checklist:** disclosure ✓ · non-fabricated testimonial ✓ (quote #1 cited, not claimed) · no fake urgency ✓ · no protected-attribute second-person framing (n/a, not a health category) ✓.

## Measurement plan

Pull Fernwell's own trailing median hook rate (3s plays ÷ impressions) for this placement as the bar to beat — don't chase a published benchmark. If hook rate clears the bar, check hold rate (15s÷3s) next; only after a hook wins, run the two CTA variants against each other in a second wave to isolate that one variable. Diagnose and fix one stage at a time — don't touch targeting or the offer until the script itself passes.
