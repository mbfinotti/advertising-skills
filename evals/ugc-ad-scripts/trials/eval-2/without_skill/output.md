# Fernwell — 20-Second UGC Ad Script

**"The Guesswork Killer"** | Vertical (9:16) | Problem-aware, first-time plant owners

Every spoken line below is locked, word-for-word — reading it exactly as written from a teleprompter fits the 20-second runtime. Bracketed notes are direction only (what to do/show), never what to say.

---

**[0:00–0:03] HOOK**
_(She's on camera, holding a slightly droopy or crispy-looking plant, straight to lens, not smiling yet — mildly exasperated.)_

> **VO:** "If you've killed more plants than you can count... this one's for you."

**On-screen text:** KILLED ANOTHER PLANT? 💀

---

**[0:03–0:07] PROBLEM**
_(She sets the sad plant down, shrugs, half-laughs at herself.)_

> **VO:** "I used to just water it and hope for the best."

**On-screen text:** "hope" is not a watering schedule

---

**[0:07–0:13] SOLUTION / DEMO**
_(Cut to her holding her phone, app open, tapping a notification/reminder on screen. Keep this beat simple — one tap, one glance up.)_

> **VO:** "Fernwell tells you exactly when to water, so you stop guessing."

**On-screen text:** it just tells you when 🪴💧

---

**[0:13–0:17] PROOF**
_(She looks back at the plant, now healthy-looking — swap in a thriving plant here, or a quick before/after cut. She's not reciting anyone else's words — the proof lives in the on-screen text, pulled directly from real reviews, attributed.)_

> **VO:** "People are telling us their plants are finally surviving — and their friends are noticing."

**On-screen text (overlay, appears and holds):**

> ⭐️⭐️⭐️⭐️⭐️ _"My plants finally aren't dying and my friends noticed."_
> — comment on our ad

---

**[0:17–0:20] CTA**
_(Straight to camera, phone in hand.)_

> **VO:** "Fernwell. Free for seven days. Link's right here."

**On-screen text (end card, holds to end):**
FERNWELL 🌿
7-day free trial
$5.99/mo after
👉 Link in bio

---

## Delivery notes for her

- **Pace:** energetic, a little fast — like texting a friend, not presenting. This is ~54 words in 20 seconds; if she runs long on a take, the natural place to breathe is right after "best" (end of Problem) and right after "guessing" (end of Solution) — not mid-sentence.
- **Tone shift:** self-deprecating/funny on the Hook and Problem → sincere and a little proud on the Proof line → brisk and clear on the CTA.
- **No line requires improvisation** — every visual beat (holding the plant, tapping the phone, the on-screen quote) is timed to run under the VO, not instead of it.

## One thing to flag before you shoot

Since she's paid per video, FTC rules require a clear paid-partnership disclosure — add **#ad** in the on-screen text or caption (not just buried in hashtags at the bottom) and in the post caption itself. Also: the VO doesn't put her own dead-monstera story in her mouth — it stays general ("I used to just water and hope") — while the specific, personal claims ("I cried when my monstera died," "it literally tells me when to water") appear as attributed on-screen quotes from real users. That keeps the ad honest without losing the emotional punch of those reviews. If you'd rather she deliver one of those quotes as her own words, she should actually be a genuine user of the app first — otherwise it reads as a testimonial she didn't live, which is the kind of thing that gets ads flagged.
