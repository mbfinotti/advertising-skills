## Diagnosis first — three of the four premises in your read are wrong

**"Hooks must be getting tired" — no evidence of that.** Fatigue shows up as hook rate *falling*. Yours is 31%, above your own trailing median of 28%. The skill's diagnostic sequence is explicit: healthy hook rate + low hold rate → the body or the hook-to-body transition is failing, not the hook. Rewriting hooks right now is fixing a stage that isn't broken.

**"TikTok hooks are way better than Reels" — not a valid comparison.** Short-form feeds count a "3-second view" at different thresholds (2s on some placements, 3s on others). A 19% vs 31% gap between platforms is very likely a measurement artifact of how each counts a play, not a creative-quality signal. Compare each placement only against its own trailing baseline for that placement — never cross-platform.

**"30%+ is elite, we're crushing it" — discard the external number.** Published "good hook rate" benchmarks range roughly 18–40% for the same metric, because each vendor sells the analytics the number justifies. The only baseline that matters is your own account history. Against that, 31% vs your 28% median is a modest, not dominant, beat — not "crushing the top."

**The actual failing stage: hold rate.** 18% against a 35% median is roughly a 50% collapse. Combined with hook rate rising, this is the textbook signature the skill names directly: *"Hook the body never pays off → hold rate collapses."* New hooks are opening a promise the current body isn't paying off in the first ~12 seconds after the 3s mark.

## Should you broaden targeting? No.

None of your three symptoms (hook rate up, hold rate down, CTR flat) live in the targeting layer — the skill's fix chain routes hook/hold/CTR problems back to the script, never to audience. Broadening now actively works against you: a wider, less-qualified audience tends to drop off *faster* mid-video, which would push hold rate down further and confound any read on whatever body fix you make. Hold targeting until hold rate is back near baseline.
